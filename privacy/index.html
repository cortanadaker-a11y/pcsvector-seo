Privacy

We collect your first name and email to match you with a military-friendly local agent for your move. Your details go to Google Forms when you open the form, and are shared with PCS Vector and the licensed agent referral partner who matches you with one local agent only when you submit it. This browser also saves your name and email to prefill the form next time.

What the calculator form collects

The fields on pcsvector.com are First name and Email. After you run the calculator, Find a home also passes the destination, rank, and number of dependents already on the screen. PCS Vector does not ask for a password, a phone number, or a mailing address on this site.

What this browser saves

If the first name and email pass the check on the button, this browser stores them in its own local storage (pcs_ot_first and pcs_ot_email) so the next visit on this device can prefill those two boxes. That storage stays on this device. Clearing site data for pcsvector.com removes it.

What is sent, and when

Opening Find a home sends your first name, email, destination, rank, and number of dependents to Google Forms in the prefilled link. PCS Vector and the licensed agent referral partner who matches you with one local agent receive them only when you press Submit. The public Google Form is titled PCS Vector and marks these questions required:

  • Email Address
  • Destination
  • First Name
  • Rank
  • Number of Dependents

The form itself says: "Never submit passwords through Google Forms." and "This content is neither created nor endorsed by Google."

Google's own notices are on the form: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The form is this page.

PCS Vector is a static site. The calculator does not run a server that receives the name or email. We don't sell your info. PCS Vector is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Army or DoW.
