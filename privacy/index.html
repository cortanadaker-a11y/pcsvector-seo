Privacy

The short form collects your first name and email. The longer Find a home form collects five fields: first name, email, destination, rank, and number of dependents. We don't sell your info.

Short form

The short form collects your first name and email.

Longer Find a home form

The longer Find a home form collects five fields: first name, email, destination, rank, and number of dependents. You type the first name and email. Destination, rank, and number of dependents come from the calculator.

What this browser saves

This browser saves the short form's first name and email in local storage (pcs_ot_first and pcs_ot_email) after they pass the check, and fills those two boxes the next time you open the calculator.

When you submit the longer form, this browser saves those details in session storage (pcs_referral_lead) until you close the tab. The saved copy also has a blank last name and the words “Not sure” for rent or buy. You are not asked those, and they are not sent to Google. The page does not read session storage back into the form.

Who receives it

Your details go to Google Forms when you open the form. They reach PCS Vector and its licensed agent referral partner, who matches you with one independent local agent, only when you submit. The form is this page.

Page counts

The site uses GoatCounter for privacy-friendly page counts. GoatCounter uses no cookies and does not receive your name or email. Page addresses may include calculator choices.
