BAH at Fort Wainwright, AK (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Wainwright. DTMO lists this housing area as FAIRBANKS, AK (code AK405). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Wainwright

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Wainwright runs about $1,750–$2,450/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Fairbanks / North Pole) run about $385–$830/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,135–$3,280/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Wainwright

Fort Wainwright housing has three Alaska-specific rules worth knowing before you travel: lodging is usually fully booked from April through September, temporary lodging allowance must be validated by the Housing Services Office every 10 days, and the acceptable off-post commute is measured in extreme winter weather. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright temporary housing; Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Wainwright

  1. Reserve lodging as soon as you have orders: Military OneSource says Alaska lodging is usually 100% booked from April through September. The Holiday Inn Express on post (Building 3402, 907-370-0999) is pet-friendly; call about pet fees. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright temporary housing)
  2. Report to the Welcome Center, Building 3401 on Santiago Road, third floor, with a copy of your orders and leave form; the line at 907-353-4311 is staffed 24 hours a day. Expect five days of in-processing before you report to your unit. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright check-in procedures; Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  3. Visit the Housing Services Office (907-353-1190) before you sign any rental or sales agreement; Military OneSource says all military members must process through it first. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  4. Request Temporary Lodging Allowance (TLA) within your first five days. Only the Housing Services Office can authorize it; it comes in 10-day increments with proof of an active housing search, for up to 20 days, and the home must be ready within 60 days of sign-in. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright temporary housing; Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright check-in procedures)
  5. Check that your PCS orders authorize the Alaska Highway (and the marine highway if you will use both); if you change your route, get the orders amended to get the right travel pay. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright check-in procedures)
  6. For your family to travel with you, orders must say concurrent travel and list each family member; each needs Exceptional Family Member screening and command sponsorship. If orders say deferred travel, you go first and start sponsorship after you arrive. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright check-in procedures)
  7. Plan to keep any commute under 30 minutes: Military OneSource defines the acceptable commute as a maximum of 30 minutes measured in extreme adverse weather (cold, ice, fog or snowstorm). (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • Off post: Military OneSource says about one-third of families assigned to the installation live off post for at least part of their tour, and that rentals are harder to get and cost more in summer than in winter. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  • Preferred Tenant Program: for soldiers living off post a year or more, participating landlords offer a 3% reduction in market rent and reduced security deposits; ask the Housing Services Office for participants. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  • Mobile homes are scarce because they are typically not insulated for extreme weather, and heating costs can rise. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  • Pets: most rentals do not accept pets, and TLA is not extended because of pet-housing difficulties; the Housing Services Office can help find pet-friendly places. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  • Utilities: Fairbanks-area utilities may waive the usual deposit; ask providers for the monthly average cost of a specific address before you lease. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  • Buying: the Alaska Housing Finance Corporation’s HomeChoice workshop is free (907-330-8250). (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)

Quick answers

Why does DTMO call it Fairbanks?

DTMO sets BAH by Military Housing Area, and Fort Wainwright sits in the FAIRBANKS, AK area (AK405). North Pole (99705) and Eielson AFB (99702) are in the same area and use the same rates.

Do I get COLA on top of BAH at Fort Wainwright?

Yes. Alaska duty stations get BAH plus a cost-of-living allowance (COLA), which DTMO sets separately by rank, years of service and dependents. This page shows BAH only; the calculator adds COLA for Fort Wainwright.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Wainwright cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,750–$2,450 and utilities of about $385–$830 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,436) lands between −$844 and $301 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Wainwright in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Wainwright with dependents receives $2,436 a month and $1,827 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,937 with and $2,301 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FAIRBANKS, AK (AK405). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

How long can I get Temporary Lodging Allowance at Fort Wainwright?

Military OneSource says TLA must be authorized within your first five days, comes in 10-day increments with proof of an aggressive housing search validated by the Housing Services Office, and is limited to 20 days to secure a rental agreement or housing offer that you can move into within 60 days of sign-in.

Does the Fort Wainwright Preferred Tenant Program save money?

Military OneSource says it offers a 3% reduction in market rent and reduced security deposits for soldiers who will live off post for a year or more, through participating providers. Ask the Housing Services Office at 907-353-1190 for the current list.
