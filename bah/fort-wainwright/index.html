BAH at Fort Wainwright, AK (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Wainwright. DTMO lists this housing area as FAIRBANKS, AK (code AK405). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Wainwright

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Wainwright runs about $1,750–$2,450/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Fairbanks / North Pole) run about $385–$830/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,135–$3,280/month.

Quick answers

Why does DTMO call it Fairbanks?

DTMO sets BAH by Military Housing Area, and Fort Wainwright sits in the FAIRBANKS, AK area (AK405). North Pole (99705) and Eielson AFB (99702) are in the same area and use the same rates.

Do I get COLA on top of BAH at Fort Wainwright?

Yes. Alaska duty stations get BAH plus a cost-of-living allowance (COLA), which DTMO sets separately by rank, years of service and dependents. This page shows BAH only; the calculator adds COLA for Fort Wainwright.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Wainwright cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,750–$2,450 and utilities of about $385–$830 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,436) lands between −$844 and $301 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
