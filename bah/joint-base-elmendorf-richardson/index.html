BAH at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson (JBER), AK (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for service members stationed at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson (also searched as JBER, Fort Richardson). DTMO lists this housing area as ANCHORAGE, AK (code AK404). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson runs about $1,900–$2,700/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Anchorage NE / Eagle River) run about $355–$750/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,255–$3,450/month.

Quick answers

Why does DTMO call it Anchorage?

DTMO sets BAH by Military Housing Area, and JBER (99506, 99505) sits in the ANCHORAGE, AK area (AK404). Anchorage (99501), Eagle River (99577) and Wasilla (99654) are in the same area and use the same rates.

Do I get COLA on top of BAH at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson?

Yes. Alaska duty stations get BAH plus a cost-of-living allowance (COLA), which DTMO sets separately by rank, years of service and dependents. This page shows BAH only; the calculator adds COLA for Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson.

Does 2026 BAH at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,900–$2,700 and utilities of about $355–$750 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,874) lands between −$576 and $619 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
