BAH at Fort Gordon, GA (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Gordon. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT GORDON, AL (code GA073). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Gordon

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Gordon runs about $1,350–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Grovetown / Augusta west (30813)) run about $255–$495/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,605–$2,245/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Gordon

Fort Gordon’s Military OneSource housing page describes an Augusta-area market with a healthy rental vacancy rate, and says the roughly 1,000 on-post family homes are all privatized. Check which county you want before you tour: Fort Gordon is in Richmond County, next to newer and pricier Columbia County. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Gordon

  1. If you want the allowed ten days of permissive TDY for house hunting, contact your gaining unit first so you have the right leave form; otherwise you are charged ordinary leave. After PTDY you report for duty at Darling Hall (Building 33720, Room 262) to start in-processing. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon check-in procedures)
  2. Budget about seven days for in-processing, which includes visits to several locations and orientations. Spouses are encouraged to attend the newcomer orientation held Thursdays at 1 p.m., except federal holidays. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon check-in procedures)
  3. Use the mobile in-processing guides Military OneSource names: the Digital Garrison and GuideOn apps, for Apple and Android. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon check-in procedures)
  4. Army personnel must have a sponsor; if you do not, contact your gaining unit. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon check-in procedures)
  5. Book lodging early: all on-post lodging is run by IHG Army Hotels (877-711-TEAM or online), with Griffith Hall just inside Gate 1. Gate 6 is open 24 hours for DOD ID cardholders. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon temporary housing)
  6. Visit the Housing Services Office (HSO) for a list of rentals with locator maps and community information; it also handles discrimination complaints and tenant-landlord disputes. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)
  7. Buy renters insurance: Military OneSource says the landlord’s policy covers only the building and that most local leases require renters insurance. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: about 1,000 family housing units, all privatized and operated by Balfour Beatty Communities (706-772-7041). (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)
  • Off post: Military OneSource says Augusta has a healthy rental vacancy rate and that security deposits are sometimes waived for military personnel, otherwise usually one month’s rent. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)
  • Pets: where pets are allowed there is usually a substantial nonrefundable deposit, and many apartments limit pets to 25 pounds (Military OneSource). (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)
  • Mobile homes are typically available and usually furnished; there is no mobile home park on the installation, so check the surroundings before signing. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)

Area notes

  • Local communities: Augusta and Hephzibah in Richmond County; Martinez, Evans and Grovetown in Columbia County. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)
  • Columbia County homes are generally newer and more expensive, and its newer schools and infrastructure can mean higher property taxes. (Military OneSource, Fort Gordon housing)

Quick answers

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Gordon cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,350–$1,750 and utilities of about $255–$495 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,890) lands between −$355 and $285 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Gordon in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Gordon with dependents receives $1,890 a month and $1,644 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,250 with and $2,058 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT GORDON, AL (GA073). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Which county should I look in near Fort Gordon?

Military OneSource says Fort Gordon is in Richmond County near the Columbia County border. Local communities include Augusta and Hephzibah in Richmond County and Martinez, Evans and Grovetown in Columbia County, where homes are generally newer and more expensive and property taxes can be higher.

Is on-post housing at Fort Gordon run by the Army?

Military OneSource says all of Fort Gordon’s family housing, roughly 1,000 units, is privatized and operated by Balfour Beatty Communities.
