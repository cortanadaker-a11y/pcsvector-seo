Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Joint Base Langley-Eustis

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Joint Base Langley-Eustis runs about $1,550–$2,150/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Hampton / Yorktown (23666)) run about $265–$515/month. PCS guide: moving to Joint Base Langley-Eustis

Joint Base Langley-Eustis has two sides in Virginia’s Hampton Roads, and each has its own housing office, privatized community and lodging. Military OneSource says Fort Eustis soldiers with dependents who apply within 30 days of signing in get the application backdated to their departure date from the last duty station. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)

PCS checklist for Joint Base Langley-Eustis

  1. Fort Eustis: contact the Housing Services Office, 650 Monroe Avenue, Room 114 (757-878-5579), before signing a lease or committing to housing. It is open Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed weekends and federal holidays. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)
  2. Apply for Fort Eustis Family Homes within 30 days of signing in: Military OneSource lists a TRICARE eligibility report showing dependents, DA Form 31, PCS orders with all amendments and, if applicable, dependent-restricted-area orders. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)
  3. Book General Smalls Inn (1700 Madison Avenue, Fort Eustis) for the first 3 to 7 nights; if it is full, ask for a Statement of Non-Availability so finance can reimburse an off-post hotel. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)
  4. In-process in duty uniform with personnel records, all PCS and DA orders, DA Form 31, and medical, dental and financial records; permanent-party soldiers at Fort Eustis report to their unit’s personnel section, which sets the appointment. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis check-in procedures)
  5. Langley side: Langley Inn has two facilities, Bayview Towers and VQ, with a check-in time of 2 p.m. and check-out at 11 a.m. Register every vehicle at the front desk; weapons are not allowed in quarters and can be stored at the base armory. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis temporary housing)
  6. Arriving at Langley after hours: report to the 24-hour Base Lodging Office, 44 Taylor Road (757-764-4667), then to your gaining unit on the next duty day. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis check-in procedures)

Housing options at a glance

  • Fort Eustis: Fort Eustis Family Homes (Balfour Beatty) has 881 homes, with its office at 126 Madison Avenue (757-369-8335); waits vary by availability, grade and bedrooms needed. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)
  • Langley: Langley Family Housing is a Hunt Military Community open to all grades, with waits depending on grade and house type. For families with special needs, Hunt works with the Exceptional Family Member Program coordinator. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)
  • Other privatized options nearby: Yorktown Naval Weapons Station is about five miles from Fort Eustis (Liberty Military Housing), and Langley Family Housing is about 15 miles away. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis housing)
  • General Smalls Inn has family suites (some pet-friendly), single rooms and six accessible rooms, with reservations by phone at any hour. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Langley-Eustis temporary housing)

Quick answers

Do Fort Eustis and Langley get the same BAH?

Yes. Fort Eustis (23604) and Langley (23665) both map to the HAMPTON/NEWPORT NEWS, VA housing area (VA297), as do Newport News (23606) and Williamsburg (23185).

Does 2026 BAH at Joint Base Langley-Eustis cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,550–$2,150 and utilities of about $265–$515 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,274) lands between −$391 and $459 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Joint Base Langley-Eustis in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Joint Base Langley-Eustis with dependents receives $2,274 a month and $1,779 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,475 with and $2,361 without. DTMO lists the housing area as HAMPTON/NEWPORT NEWS, VA (VA297). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

When should I apply for family housing at Fort Eustis?

Military OneSource says incoming personnel should apply within 30 days of signing into Fort Eustis so the application is backdated to the departure date from the last permanent duty station.

What if General Smalls Inn is full?

Military OneSource says to ask the inn for a Statement of Non-Availability, which lets finance reimburse a hotel off post, and that the inn may provide a list of lodging sites.
