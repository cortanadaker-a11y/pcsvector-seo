BAH at Joint Base San Antonio (JBSA), TX (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for service members stationed at Joint Base San Antonio (also searched as JBSA, Fort Sam Houston, Lackland, Randolph). DTMO lists this housing area as SAN ANTONIO, TX (code TX285). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Joint Base San Antonio

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Joint Base San Antonio runs about $1,400–$1,950/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; San Antonio NE / Schertz / Universal City) run about $255–$500/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,655–$2,450/month.

PCS guide: moving to JBSA (Fort Sam Houston, Lackland, Randolph)

Joint Base San Antonio combines Fort Sam Houston, Lackland and Randolph. One DTMO housing area covers all three, so a search for “Fort Sam Houston BAH” and one for “JBSA BAH” return the same rate.

PCS checklist for JBSA (Fort Sam Houston, Lackland, Randolph)

  1. Contact the Military Housing Office (MHO) for the campus you report to: JBSA-Fort Sam Houston 210-221-0881, JBSA-Lackland 210-671-1840, JBSA-Randolph 830-483-9900. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)
  2. The Fort Sam Houston MHO is at 3171 Zinn Road, Building 1161; hours listed by Military OneSource are Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)
  3. Use the MHO for rental searches and for lease review and disputes; Military OneSource describes it as the subject-matter expert on leases, discrimination complaints and tenant-landlord disputes. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)
  4. Search HOMES.mil by ZIP code: listings are reviewed by the Military Housing Office for discriminatory content and safety concerns before they are approved. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)
  5. Carry renters insurance: the property owner’s policy covers the building but not your belongings. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)
  6. Look up utility providers by ZIP code before you sign so you can compare deposits and connection fees. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • Military OneSource says the San Antonio area has over 174 neighborhoods, with rents that vary considerably by style, size and location. (Military OneSource, JBSA housing)
  • On-post family housing at Fort Sam Houston is privatized; see the garrison’s privatized Army housing page for how to apply. (JBSA-Fort Sam Houston privatized Army housing)

Quick answers

Do Fort Sam Houston, Lackland and Randolph all get the same BAH?

Yes. ZIPs 78234 (JBSA-Fort Sam Houston), 78236 (JBSA-Lackland) and 78150 (JBSA-Randolph) all map to the SAN ANTONIO, TX housing area (TX285), so all three use these rates.

Does 2026 BAH at Joint Base San Antonio cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,400–$1,950 and utilities of about $255–$500 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,869) lands between −$581 and $214 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Sam Houston in 2026?

Fort Sam Houston, Lackland and Randolph are all in DTMO’s SAN ANTONIO, TX housing area (TX285). In 2026 an E-5 with dependents receives $1,869 and an E-5 without dependents $1,500; the full table by grade is on this page.
