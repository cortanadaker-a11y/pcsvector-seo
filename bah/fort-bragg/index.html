BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Bragg

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Bragg runs about $1,450–$1,850/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Hope Mills / Fayetteville (28348)) run about $270–$515/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,720–$2,365/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Bragg

Fort Bragg’s housing office makes a simple rule explicit: every service member, married or single, reports to the Housing Services Office before signing a rental, lease or purchase agreement off post. (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Bragg

  1. Report to the Housing Services Office (HSO), Soldier Support Center, Normandy Drive, Building 4-2843, basement level (BH2) near the elevators. Phone 910-396-1022; Military OneSource lists Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., Tuesday from 9 a.m., closed 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)
  2. Ask for rental listings, maps, landlord-tenant responsibilities, North Carolina rental law information and the off-limits establishment list, all provided at the information briefings. (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)
  3. For on-post family housing, the privatized manager is Corvias Military Housing (leasing office, 2850 Compass Court, 910-495-0878). Wait times vary with rank and bedroom count; applications are not complete without supporting documents. (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)
  4. Single Soldiers in the rank of Staff Sergeant and below must live in the barracks, which also applies to Soldiers married to Soldiers at that rank when the spouse is stationed elsewhere; Sergeant First Class and above may live off post or ask about Randolph Point (910-764-5008). (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Corvias communities are organized into enclaves for particular rank bands, from junior enlisted through field-grade officers. (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)
  • Off post: the HSO is the contact for discrimination complaints, tenant-landlord disputes and off-post housing questions. (Military OneSource, Fort Bragg housing)

Quick answers

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Bragg cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,450–$1,850 and utilities of about $270–$515 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,806) lands between −$559 and $86 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Bragg in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Bragg with dependents receives $1,806 a month and $1,527 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,175 with and $1,956 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT BRAGG/POPE, NC (NC182). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.
