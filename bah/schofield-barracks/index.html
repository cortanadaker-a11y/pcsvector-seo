BAH at Schofield Barracks, HI (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Schofield Barracks. DTMO lists this housing area as HONOLULU COUNTY, HI (code HI408). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Schofield Barracks

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Schofield Barracks runs about $2,500–$3,500/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Wahiawa / Central Oahu) run about $320–$610/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,820–$4,110/month.

PCS guide: moving to Schofield Barracks

Schofield Barracks is in a very high cost area, and Military OneSource requires every member with dependents to visit their Housing Services Office before renting, leasing or buying on or off post. Army housing on Oahu has been privatized since 2004 through Island Palm Communities. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)

PCS checklist for Schofield Barracks

  1. Report to the Housing Services Office within five days of arrival and before renting, leasing, buying or making any housing arrangement. Military OneSource adds that you must report to housing within seven workdays of your reporting date even if you do not plan to live on post. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  2. Contact the Housing Service Office (808-787-7700) about Temporary Lodging Allowance, lodging and government housing; it gives you a list of TLA-approved facilities. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter temporary housing)
  3. Apply at the regional leasing office: North at Building 950, 215 Duck Road on Schofield Barracks (808-275-3700), or South at Building 1004, 111 Seventh Street on Fort Shafter (808-275-3800). Your application is processed and you are added to the waiting list. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  4. When you land at Daniel K. Inouye International Airport, go to the Army replacement liaison desk in front of Baggage Claim 19 to begin in-processing. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter check-in procedures)
  5. Hand-carry: PCS orders with all amendments, command sponsorship orders for family members, DA Form 31, your travel itinerary, your personnel file and medical and dental records. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter check-in procedures)
  6. Budget for move-in: Military OneSource says landlords generally want the first month’s rent plus a deposit of one month’s rent, utilities are usually not included, and utility deposit waivers are usually granted to military personnel. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  7. Contact the housing office for a pre-move assessment on what to ship and what to store: Hawaii is a full weight allowance area, but Military OneSource notes housing there is generally smaller than in the continental U.S. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • Island Palm Communities’ North region covers Schofield Barracks, Wheeler Army Airfield and Helemano Military Reservation; the South region covers Fort Shafter, Tripler Army Medical Center, Aliamanu Military Reservation and Red Hill. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  • Military OneSource reports 7,910 homes in total and says there is normally a wait on every waiting list, so check with the leasing office for current availability. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  • Wait times: for enlisted families from immediate to one month, with officer housing up to the 60-day TLA limit; June through August is the peak season. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  • Pets: homes are pet-friendly with a two-pet maximum per family and certain restrictions, plus one-time move-in and move-out charges. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  • There are no mobile homes in Hawaii. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)
  • The Inn at Schofield is a pet-friendly lodging option with single and double rooms; ADA rooms can be requested at 808-839-2336. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter temporary housing)

Quick answers

Do all Oahu posts get the same BAH?

Yes. Schofield Barracks (96857), Wahiawa (96786), Fort Shafter (96858), Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam (96853, 96860) and Marine Corps Base Hawaii (96863) all map to HONOLULU COUNTY, HI (HI408).

Do I get COLA on top of BAH at Schofield Barracks?

Yes. Hawaii duty stations get BAH plus a cost-of-living allowance (COLA), which DTMO sets separately by rank, years of service and dependents. This page shows BAH only; the calculator adds COLA for Schofield Barracks.

Does 2026 BAH at Schofield Barracks cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $2,500–$3,500 and utilities of about $320–$610 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($3,663) lands between −$447 and $843 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Schofield Barracks in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Schofield Barracks with dependents receives $3,663 a month and $2,856 without dependents; an O-3 receives $4,428 with and $3,819 without. DTMO lists the housing area as HONOLULU COUNTY, HI (HI408). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

How soon must I visit housing after arriving at Schofield Barracks?

Military OneSource says members with dependents must report to their Housing Services Office within five days of arrival, before renting or buying, and must also report to housing within seven workdays of their reporting date even if they do not plan to live on post.

Who manages family housing at Schofield Barracks?

Military OneSource says Island Palm Communities manages privatized Army family housing on Oahu, in a North region (including Schofield Barracks) and a South region (including Fort Shafter), and that Army housing on the island has been privatized since 2004.
