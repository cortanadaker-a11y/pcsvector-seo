BAH at Fort Bliss, TX (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Bliss. DTMO lists this housing area as EL PASO, TX (code TX279). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Bliss

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Typical off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Bliss runs about $1,200–$1,650/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Northeast El Paso / 79938) run about $215–$430/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,415–$2,080/month.

Quick answers

Does living in New Mexico change my Fort Bliss BAH?

No. Chaparral, NM (88081) sits in a different DTMO housing area (NM209), but that doesn’t matter: BAH follows your duty-station ZIP, and Fort Bliss is in the EL PASO, TX area (TX279). You get the El Paso rate wherever you rent.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Bliss cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With typical 3-bedroom rent of $1,200–$1,650 and utilities of about $215–$430 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,809) lands between −$271 and $394 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
