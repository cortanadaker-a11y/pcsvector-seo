BAH at Fort Bliss, TX (2026)

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Bliss

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Bliss runs about $1,200–$1,650/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Northeast El Paso / 79938) run about $215–$430/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,415–$2,080/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Bliss

Fort Bliss is in El Paso, Texas, and DTMO prices it as the EL PASO housing area. On-post family housing is privatized, and the installation tells arriving members to see its Housing Services Office before arranging anything off post. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Bliss

  1. Report to the Housing Services Office (HSO), Building 515B, Pershing Road, before you rent, lease or buy off post. Military OneSource lists hours of Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to noon and 1 to 4 p.m., closed the third Thursday of each month from noon to 4 p.m.; phone 915-568-2898. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)
  2. Do not send deposits for housing before you have contacted the HSO. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)
  3. Service members are told to report to the HSO at both their current and new duty stations before making off-post arrangements, so call your losing installation’s office too. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)
  4. If you want on-post family housing, contact Fort Bliss Family Homes (1991 Marshall Road, El Paso): 915-564-0795 for leasing, with two-, three- and four-bedroom homes. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Fort Bliss Family Homes is a gated, privatized community with pools, playgrounds and sport courts, and is described as open first to active-duty families assigned to the base, with select homes for retirees, DoW civilians and the public. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)
  • Off post: the HSO is the office to ask for rental listings and housing help before you pay any deposit. (Military OneSource, Fort Bliss housing)

Quick answers

Does living in New Mexico change my Fort Bliss BAH?

No. Chaparral, NM (88081) sits in a different DTMO housing area (NM209), but that doesn’t matter: BAH follows your duty-station ZIP, and Fort Bliss is in the EL PASO, TX area (TX279). You get the El Paso rate wherever you rent.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Bliss cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,200–$1,650 and utilities of about $215–$430 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,809) lands between −$271 and $394 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Bliss in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Bliss with dependents receives $1,809 a month and $1,437 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,202 with and $2,022 without. DTMO lists the housing area as EL PASO, TX (TX279). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.
