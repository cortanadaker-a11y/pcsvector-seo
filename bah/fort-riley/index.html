BAH at Fort Riley, KS (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Riley. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT RILEY, KS (code KS100). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Riley

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Riley runs about $1,150–$1,650/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Junction City (66441)) run about $255–$520/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,405–$2,170/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Riley

Fort Riley’s on-post family housing is privatized and assigned by rank and bedroom qualification, and Military OneSource warns that being on the waitlist does not guarantee a home when you arrive. Plan your first weeks around that. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Riley

  1. Apply for on-post housing as soon as you know you are coming; Military OneSource says service members may apply as soon as they know they will be assigned. Applying within 30 days of signing in preserves a backdated effective date to your detachment date. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  2. Secure temporary lodging before you arrive and be ready to pay for lodging beyond what the Temporary Lodging Expense covers until adequate housing is found. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  3. Gather the on-post application documents: orders to Fort Riley, a DD 93 or DD 1172 that is less than a year old (no handwritten copies), and a DA 31 if you took leave. Dual-military, single pregnant, restricted-tour and power-of-attorney cases have extra requirements listed on the source page. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  4. Corvias Military Living’s leasing office is at 45 Barry Avenue (Main Post), 785-717-2258, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  5. For off-post housing, use the Army Housing Office; Military OneSource says it may be able to help get a deposit waived, depending on the property owner. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  6. Do not treat an on-post offer as a reason to break a lease: the installation states that accepting on-post housing is not justification to break a lease or rental agreement. For lease questions when you hold deployment orders, call the Off Post Housing Division, 785-239-3525. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: five neighborhoods managed by Corvias Military Living; homes are assigned by rank and bedroom qualification and wait times vary. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  • Pets on post: at most two dogs or cats per household; several breeds are not allowed on Fort Riley or in family housing, so read the pet policy before you commit. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)
  • Boats and RVs may be kept in a family-housing garage with the door closed, or in the free (often full) Camp Forsyth RV lot with a waiting list. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)

Quick answers

Do Junction City and Manhattan get the same BAH?

Yes. Junction City (66441) and Manhattan (66502) are both in the FORT RILEY, KS housing area (KS100). BAH follows your duty-station ZIP anyway, not the town you rent in.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Riley cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,150–$1,650 and utilities of about $255–$520 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,314) lands between −$856 and −$91 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Riley in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Riley with dependents receives $1,314 a month and $1,158 without dependents; an O-3 receives $1,953 with and $1,611 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT RILEY, KS (KS100). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.
