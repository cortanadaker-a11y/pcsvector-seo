Quick answers

Does BAH change if I live in a different ZIP than my post?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address. Look up the ZIP on your orders.

What is 2026 BAH for ZIP 22204?

22204 is in the Washington, DC metro housing area (DC053) on the National Capital Region page. In 2026 an E-5 with dependents receives $3,132 and an O-6 with dependents receives $4,731.

What is 2026 BAH for ZIP 22030?

22030 is in the same Washington, DC metro housing area (DC053) as 22204. The 2026 rate is the same: $3,132 for an E-5 with dependents and $4,731 for an O-6 with dependents.

What if my ZIP is not listed?

This lookup only includes ZIP codes published on the 27 Army post pages. It does not invent a rate. Use the DTMO BAH Rate Lookup for any other duty-station ZIP.
