BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Campbell

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Typical off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Campbell runs about $1,350–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Clarksville / 37042) run about $265–$520/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,615–$2,270/month.

Quick answers

Do I get a different rate if I live in Clarksville, TN?

No. Fort Campbell straddles Kentucky and Tennessee, but BAH follows your duty-station ZIP (housing area KY106), not which side of the state line you rent on. State taxes and utilities do differ, so check those before you sign.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Campbell cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With typical 3-bedroom rent of $1,350–$1,750 and utilities of about $265–$520 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,815) lands between −$455 and $200 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
