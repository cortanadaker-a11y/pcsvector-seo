BAH at Fort Leonard Wood, MO (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Leonard Wood. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT LEONARD WOOD, MO (code MO163). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Leonard Wood

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Leonard Wood runs about $1,100–$1,550/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; St. Robert (65584)) run about $250–$510/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,350–$2,060/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Leonard Wood

Fort Leonard Wood’s family housing is privatized and open to all ranks, in about 1,800 two-, three- and four-bedroom homes in four neighborhoods run by Balfour Beatty. Military OneSource’s page also spells out who must live off post, which decides whether you ever compare a rent against BAH. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Leonard Wood

  1. Active-duty Army personnel report to the Central In/Out Processing Office, Building 470, Room 2105, from 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Soldiers arriving with family members go first to the Lodging Office inside Candlewood Suites (Building 2020), open 24 hours. Marine Corps, Navy and Air Force personnel have separate reporting points listed on Military OneSource. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood check-in procedures)
  2. Pack the documents Military OneSource lists: passports, driver’s license and military ID, naturalization papers if relevant, power of attorney, credit card information, tax records, school transcripts, employment records and references, insurance policies, car titles and registration, and sales receipts for furniture and high-value items. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood check-in procedures)
  3. Reserve lodging early: PCS families can book 60 days before arrival through the IHG Army Hotels lodging office at 3400 Nebraska Avenue (573-586-4800 or 800-677-8356). (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood temporary housing)
  4. Watch the stay limit: Military OneSource says the maximum length of stay is 30 days without forfeiture of BAH. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood temporary housing)
  5. If you have pets, join the waiting list early: eight cottages on Community Drive take pets, and the page says there is a 30-day waiting list. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood temporary housing)
  6. Contact the Housing Referral Office for off-post help: it gives you rental lists with locator maps and community information and handles discrimination complaints and tenant-landlord disputes. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood housing)
  7. Call the housing office at 573-329-0122 about on-post family housing, and carry renters insurance wherever you live off post. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Balfour Beatty took over family housing in June 2008; Military OneSource cites about 1,806 families and describes about 1,800 homes in four neighborhoods managed by Fort Leonard Wood Homes. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood housing)
  • Single soldiers: E-1 to E-3 are assigned space in Specker Barracks, E-4 to E-5 in renovated family quarters on Indiana Avenue, and E-6 to E-9 must live off post. Soldiers with legally supported family members and geographic bachelors also live off post. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood housing)
  • There are no permanent-party bachelor officer quarters on post. (Military OneSource, Fort Leonard Wood housing)

Quick answers

Do St. Robert, Waynesville and Rolla get the same BAH?

Yes. St. Robert (65584), Waynesville (65583) and Rolla (65401) are all in the FORT LEONARD WOOD, MO housing area (MO163). BAH follows your duty-station ZIP anyway, not the town you rent in.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Leonard Wood cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,100–$1,550 and utilities of about $250–$510 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,479) lands between −$581 and $129 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Leonard Wood in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Leonard Wood with dependents receives $1,479 a month and $1,290 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,028 with and $1,629 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT LEONARD WOOD, MO (MO163). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Who has to live off post at Fort Leonard Wood?

Military OneSource says all personnel in grades E-6 to E-9 must reside off post, along with soldiers who have legally supported family members and geographic bachelors. Single soldiers in E-1 to E-5 are assigned post barracks or renovated family quarters.

Will long lodging stays cost me BAH at Fort Leonard Wood?

Military OneSource says the maximum length of stay for PCS lodging at Fort Leonard Wood is 30 days without forfeiture of BAH. Ask the lodging office and your finance office how that applies to your orders.
