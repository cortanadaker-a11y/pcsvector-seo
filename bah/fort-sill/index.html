BAH at Fort Sill, OK (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Sill. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT SILL/LAWTON, OK (code OK237). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Sill

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Sill runs about $950–$1,300/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Lawton west (73505)) run about $240–$470/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,190–$1,770/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Sill

Fort Sill sits north of Lawton, Oklahoma, about 90 miles southwest of Oklahoma City. Military OneSource says about 80% of military personnel in the Fort Sill area live off post, so the BAH-versus-rent question matters more than the on-post waitlist. (Fort Sill newcomers page; Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Sill

  1. In-process at the Welcome Center, Building 4700 on Mow-Way Road. Bring your PCS orders; in-processing takes 3 to 5 days. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill check-in procedures; Fort Sill newcomers page)
  2. Temporary lodging: unaccompanied E-5 and below get a temporary room; accompanied members and E-6 and above check with on-post lodging (IHG, Building 5676), and if no room is free you receive a statement of non-availability that allows off-post lodging in Lawton. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill check-in procedures)
  3. Before you rent, lease or buy off post, report to the Housing Services Office (HSO), Welcome Center, Building 4700, third floor. Military OneSource says you are required to report in person before making any off-post housing arrangements. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing; Fort Sill newcomers page)
  4. Ask the HSO for its list of rentals with locator maps and information on the surrounding communities. The newcomers page says it provides referrals, landlord mediation, fair housing information and Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) guidance. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing; Fort Sill newcomers page)
  5. Relocation help: the Army Community Service relocation program is on the first floor of Building 4700 (PCS planning, area orientations, settlement assistance). (Fort Sill newcomers page)
  6. Budget the move-in costs: security deposits at Fort Sill vary and often equal one month’s rent, and the installation advises every renter to carry renters insurance because the owner’s policy does not cover your belongings. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • Off post: about 80% of military personnel live off post in the Fort Sill area, in apartment complexes, mobile homes and houses for rent or sale in Lawton and nearby towns. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing)
  • On post: family housing is privatized through Corvias Property Management (apply through its website, sill.corviaspm.com). (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing; Fort Sill newcomers page)
  • Single E-1 to E-5 members are required to occupy barracks space; living off post at the single rate needs an approved exception to policy and a Certificate of Non-Availability. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing)

Quick answers

Why does DTMO call it Fort Sill/Lawton?

DTMO sets BAH by Military Housing Area, and Fort Sill and the city of Lawton share one area, FORT SILL/LAWTON, OK (OK237). Lawton ZIPs such as 73505 use the same rates.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Sill cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $950–$1,300 and utilities of about $240–$470 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,233) lands between −$537 and $43 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Sill in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Sill with dependents receives $1,233 a month and $1,092 without dependents; an O-3 receives $1,758 with and $1,395 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT SILL/LAWTON, OK (OK237). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Is Fort Sill BAH the same as BAH in Oklahoma City or Tulsa?

No. DTMO sets BAH by military housing area, and Fort Sill’s area (FORT SILL/LAWTON, OK, OK237) covers only the Lawton-area ZIP codes listed on this page. Oklahoma City, Tulsa and other Oklahoma ZIP codes belong to different housing areas, so look them up in DTMO’s BAH calculator instead of using these rates.
