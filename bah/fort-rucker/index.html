BAH at Fort Rucker, AL (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Rucker. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT RUCKER, AL (code AL002). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Rucker

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Rucker runs about $1,150–$1,650/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Enterprise / Daleville (36330)) run about $250–$490/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,400–$2,140/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Rucker

Fort Rucker’s family housing is entirely on post and entirely privatized, and Military OneSource says there are no other government housing options such as leased homes in the community. It also says you should apply as soon as you receive orders, because availability drops in peak PCS season. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Rucker

  1. Apply for on-post housing as soon as your orders arrive, using the Corvias online application or the Leasing and Relocation Center at 9700 Division Road (334-440-8988). Military OneSource notes availability is lower in peak PCS season. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)
  2. Sign in during duty hours (7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday to Friday) at the Soldier Service Center, Military Personnel Division, Building 5700, Room 280, with PCS orders and your leave authorization form. Military OneSource recommends reporting during duty hours. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker check-in procedures)
  3. Arriving after hours, on a weekend or on a holiday: report to Building 101 (the installation operations center, 334-255-3100), then go to Building 5700, Room 280 at 7:30 a.m. the first duty day in duty uniform. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker check-in procedures)
  4. Visit the Housing Services Office (334-255-2224) in the Soldier Service Center, Building 5700, Room 160; Military OneSource calls it the soldier’s advocate. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)
  5. Set up utilities at the Utilities Clearing House (Building 5700, Room 190, 334-255-3554), which helps soldiers and families with electricity and cable service. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)
  6. Reserve lodging: IHG Army Hotels is in Building 308 on Sixth Avenue (877-711-TEAM or 334-598-5216), about 20 miles from Dothan Regional Airport. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker temporary housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Corvias Military Living runs three neighborhood community centers (pool, fitness center, club room, computer lab) that residents can use at no extra cost, plus 24-hour maintenance, lawn care, pest control and refuse pickup. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)
  • Eligibility: accompanied and unaccompanied service members, geographic bachelors, federal civilians and military retirees can apply; children are eligible to attend DoWEA schools. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)
  • Single members: two- and three-bedroom homes are available to E-6 and above and are shared by two service members of the same rank and gender; unaccompanied personnel housing for staff sergeants and above is voluntary and first come, first served. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker housing)
  • Pets in lodging: IHG charges a nonrefundable pet fee plus a daily fee starting the sixth night. (Military OneSource, Fort Rucker temporary housing)

Quick answers

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Rucker cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,150–$1,650 and utilities of about $250–$490 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,572) lands between −$568 and $172 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Rucker in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Rucker with dependents receives $1,572 a month and $1,251 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,262 with and $1,767 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT RUCKER, AL (AL002). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Is any government housing available off post near Fort Rucker?

No. Military OneSource says there is no government-owned or leased housing in the community and no government-operated mobile home park; all government housing is on the installation and is privatized.

Which towns are near Fort Rucker?

Military OneSource’s Fort Rucker housing page says the post is in the Wiregrass area, with Daleville and Ozark in Dale County, Enterprise in Coffee County and Dothan in Houston County.
