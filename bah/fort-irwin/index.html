BAH at Fort Irwin, CA (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Irwin (also searched as National Training Center (NTC)). DTMO lists this housing area as BARSTOW/FORT IRWIN, CA (code CA028). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Irwin

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Irwin runs about $1,100–$1,600/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Barstow (92311)) run about $270–$540/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,370–$2,140/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Irwin

Fort Irwin sits far from the nearest large town, and Military OneSource says most families prefer to live on post because of the distance to Barstow and the extra cost of living there. On-post homes are often not available on arrival, so apply 60 to 90 days before your PCS date. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Irwin

  1. Report to the Replacement Detachment, Building 109, Langford Lake Road (open 24 hours) with your leave form, finance records, medical and dental records and five copies of your orders. After duty hours use the Command Duty Section, Building 988. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin check-in procedures)
  2. Attend the Mandatory Newcomers Briefing, held every Friday at the Replacement Detachment; family members are encouraged to come. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin check-in procedures)
  3. Apply for on-post housing 60 to 90 days before your PCS date. Military OneSource says the waiting list varies from 1 to 6 months. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  4. If you must wait, ask the privatized housing partner to move you into a temporary home at a fixed BAH rate until your home is ready. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  5. Arriving from an unaccompanied tour such as Korea earns a waiting-period adjustment equal to the months served, up to 18 months. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  6. Lodging: the Landmark Inn (760-386-4040) is the only hotel within 35 miles of Fort Irwin. Military OneSource says it is often sold out months in advance during rotations, and that as a private business it does not set priorities for who may reserve. It also says a non-availability statement is not required to be reimbursed if you stay in a Barstow motel, so keep your receipts. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin temporary housing)
  7. Families staying in Barstow, Victorville or Apple Valley can use the satellite Army Community Service center, the Military Connection and Resource Center at 512 Barstow Road (760-256-5751). Both it and the on-post ACS have a lending closet with kitchenware, microwaves, card tables and some baby items. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin check-in procedures)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: family housing is privatized; Michaels Management Services has been the community partner since July 2015, and leasing is at 4553 Tippecanoe in the Sandy Basin community. Homes have 2 to 4 bedrooms and some are accessible. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  • How BAH works on post: because housing is privatized, the full BAH amount shows on your LES as an entitlement and is simultaneously taken out as an allotment paid to the landlord for rent. Utilities except telephone and cable are included in the rent within the median baseline. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  • Off post: Military OneSource says apartment rents are plus utilities (it gives an undated range, so ask for current prices) and that the Housing Services Office can name reputable complexes and property managers. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  • Single members: company grade officers, warrant officers and soldiers E-6 and above drawing BAH at the without-dependents rate are not required to live on post; unaccompanied housing is available through the private partner. (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)
  • There is no mobile home park at Fort Irwin, but there is an RV site (Outdoor Recreation, 760-380-4327). (Military OneSource, Fort Irwin housing)

Quick answers

Why does DTMO call it Barstow/Fort Irwin?

DTMO places Fort Irwin and the National Training Center in the BARSTOW/FORT IRWIN, CA housing area (CA028), so Barstow (92311) rentals use the same rates.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Irwin cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,100–$1,600 and utilities of about $270–$540 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,001) lands between −$139 and $631 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Irwin in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Irwin with dependents receives $2,001 a month and $1,695 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,748 with and $2,274 without. DTMO lists the housing area as BARSTOW/FORT IRWIN, CA (CA028). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Why does my full BAH go to rent on post at Fort Irwin?

Military OneSource says Fort Irwin’s on-post housing is privatized, so your full BAH appears on your LES as an entitlement and is subtracted at the same time as an allotment paid to the housing partner for rent. Most utilities are included in the rent within the median baseline.

How long is the wait for on-post housing at Fort Irwin?

Military OneSource says it varies from 1 to 6 months, and recommends applying 60 to 90 days before your PCS date. If you have to wait, you can ask the housing partner for a temporary home at a fixed BAH rate.
