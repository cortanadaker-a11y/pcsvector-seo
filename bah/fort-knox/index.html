BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Knox

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Typical off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Knox runs about $1,250–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Radcliff / Elizabethtown (40160)) run about $260–$520/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,510–$2,270/month.

Quick answers

Do Radcliff and Elizabethtown get the same BAH?

Yes. Radcliff (40160) and Elizabethtown (42701) are both in the FORT KNOX, KY housing area (KY110). BAH follows your duty-station ZIP anyway, not the town you rent in.

Does 2027 BAH at Fort Knox cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With typical 3-bedroom rent of $1,250–$1,750 and utilities of about $260–$520 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,647) lands between −$623 and $137 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2027 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
