BAH at Fort Knox, KY (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Knox. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT KNOX, KY (code KY110). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Knox

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Knox runs about $1,250–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Radcliff / Elizabethtown (40160)) run about $260–$520/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,510–$2,270/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Knox

Military OneSource’s Fort Knox housing page says all incoming soldiers must first report to Housing Services to in-process housing, and that the same office checks that leases carry military provisions. Its check-in page separately tells soldiers to report to their gaining unit on day one. On-post family housing is privatized and run by Knox Hills. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing; Military OneSource, Fort Knox check-in procedures)

PCS checklist for Fort Knox

  1. Day one: report to your gaining unit so your arrival is recorded in IPPS-A; Military OneSource’s check-in page says to do this before the Process Control Station. If you arrive after duty hours, sign in at the Department of Emergency Services, Building 298 on Gold Vault Road (502-624-6847). (Military OneSource, Fort Knox check-in procedures)
  2. Housing: Military OneSource’s housing page says all incoming soldiers must first report to Housing Services (Housing Division Office, Building 1110A, Suite 101, Sixth Avenue) to in-process housing under AR 420-1, and that counselors give referrals and a local orientation. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)
  3. Before you rent, lease or buy off post, visit or contact the Housing Services Office (502-624-8084). Military OneSource says it can help you avoid scams, unreliable realtors and unsuitable rentals, and checks that leases have the provisions military renters need. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)
  4. Search HOMES.mil, the joint military housing listings site; Military OneSource says it marks properties approved by HSO staff and lists Rental Partnership Program offers, and that you can search without an account. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)
  5. Then complete in-processing in duty uniform: report to the Process Control Station at Rockenbach Hall, Building 2020, Room 130 no later than one duty day after your unit completes your arrival transaction, with your orders, any amendments and your corrected absence request. Processing hours are 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Monday to Thursday and 8:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. Friday, closed 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox check-in procedures)
  6. You then get five duty days to visit the in-processing agencies and return to finalize your checklist. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox check-in procedures)
  7. Lodging: IHG Army Hotels, 406 N. Knox Street (502-943-1000), has five facilities and a front desk open 24 hours; Military OneSource strongly encourages reservations. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox temporary housing)
  8. Single and unaccompanied soldiers at E-5 and below contact the Housing Division Office at 502-624-8084. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Knox Hills LLC owns, operates and maintains all family housing. The leasing office is at 41 W. Chaffee Avenue (502-799-6550, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.); maintenance is at 144 S. Conroy Avenue (502-799-6565). (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)
  • Knox Hills has three community offices: Historic (41 W. Chaffee Avenue), North and South Dietz (75 Estrada Avenue) and Oak Park (101 S. Brett). (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)
  • Off post: the HSO provides listings plus local school-district information and county planning and zoning rules. (Military OneSource, Fort Knox housing)

Quick answers

Do Radcliff and Elizabethtown get the same BAH?

Yes. Radcliff (40160) and Elizabethtown (42701) are both in the FORT KNOX, KY housing area (KY110). BAH follows your duty-station ZIP anyway, not the town you rent in.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Knox cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,250–$1,750 and utilities of about $260–$520 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,647) lands between −$623 and $137 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Knox in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Knox with dependents receives $1,647 a month and $1,443 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,421 with and $1,956 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT KNOX, KY (KY110). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Who owns the family housing at Fort Knox?

Military OneSource says Fort Knox’s on-post housing is privatized and that Knox Hills LLC owns, operates and maintains all of the family housing. The leasing office is at 41 W. Chaffee Avenue.

Where do I report first at Fort Knox?

Military OneSource’s check-in page says to report to your gaining unit on day one, then to the Process Control Station no later than one duty day after your arrival is recorded; after hours, sign in at the Department of Emergency Services (Building 298). Its housing page says all incoming soldiers must first report to Housing Services to in-process housing, and must visit or contact it before renting, leasing or buying off post.
