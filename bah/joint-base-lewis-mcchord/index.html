BAH vs rent and utilities at Joint Base Lewis-McChord

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Typical off-post 3-bedroom rent near Joint Base Lewis-McChord runs about $2,000–$2,600/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; DuPont (98327)) run about $275–$520/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,275–$3,120/month.

Quick answers

Is JBLM BAH the same as Tacoma BAH?

Yes. DTMO places Joint Base Lewis-McChord in the TACOMA, WA housing area (WA311), so Lewis Main, Lewis North and McChord Field all use these rates.

Does 2027 BAH at Joint Base Lewis-McChord cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With typical 3-bedroom rent of $2,000–$2,600 and utilities of about $275–$520 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,556) lands between −$564 and $281 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2027 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
