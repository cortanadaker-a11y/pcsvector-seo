BAH vs rent and utilities at Joint Base Lewis-McChord

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Joint Base Lewis-McChord runs about $2,000–$2,600/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; DuPont (98327)) run about $275–$520/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,275–$3,120/month.

PCS guide: moving to Joint Base Lewis-McChord

Joint Base Lewis-McChord has one of the larger privatized housing communities in the Army, and Military OneSource says you pick a community when you apply and are placed on the wait list by rank, bedroom need and the communities you chose. The Housing Services Office also runs a Rental Partnership Program for off-base renters. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)

PCS checklist for Joint Base Lewis-McChord

  1. Apply for on-base housing on the Liberty Military Housing website (active-duty members or their spouses); you must choose a community at application, and a leasing specialist is at 360-858-6097. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)
  2. Keep the Liberty leasing office informed of changes in eligibility, unit, duty and phone numbers; Military OneSource says that is your responsibility. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)
  3. Request a housing welcome packet before you arrive by emailing the Housing Services Office at the address on Military OneSource’s housing page. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)
  4. Report to the Installation Reception Center, Building 2021 on Pendleton Avenue (Lewis Main), regardless of unit assignment. If you will be late, call 253-967-7004. Expect about five days with the reception detachment. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord check-in procedures)
  5. Bring your orders, a DA Form 31 or IPPS-A absence request, and medical and dental records. Copies are made at the reception desk on arrival. Members returning to active duty after a break in service bring additional papers listed on the page. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord check-in procedures)
  6. Accompanied members arrange their own lodging at Rainier Inn on Lewis Main (253-964-0211) or Candlewood Suites on McChord Field (253-300-9200); both are pet-friendly. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord check-in procedures; Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord temporary housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On base: Liberty Military Housing offers more than 5,370 homes in 23 communities, reserved for active-duty service members, with wait times in every community. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)
  • Single soldiers: unaccompanied soldiers E-5 and below are required to live in the barracks and in-process with the barracks manager. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)
  • Off base: the Rental Partnership Program asks landlords to give a discount of 5% or more on monthly rent and to waive security or rent deposits and credit-check fees. Call 253-967-3581. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)
  • The Housing Services Office can brief you on your rights under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act, answer lease questions, mediate landlord disputes, hold home-buying workshops and counsel BAH-eligible personnel before they sign a lease or purchase. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Lewis-McChord housing)

Quick answers

Is JBLM BAH the same as Tacoma BAH?

Yes. DTMO places Joint Base Lewis-McChord in the TACOMA, WA housing area (WA311), so Lewis Main, Lewis North and McChord Field all use these rates.

Does 2026 BAH at Joint Base Lewis-McChord cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $2,000–$2,600 and utilities of about $275–$520 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,556) lands between −$564 and $281 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Joint Base Lewis-McChord in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Joint Base Lewis-McChord with dependents receives $2,556 a month and $2,121 without dependents; an O-3 receives $3,123 with and $2,784 without. DTMO lists the housing area as TACOMA, WA (WA311). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Who has to live in the barracks at Joint Base Lewis-McChord?

Military OneSource says unaccompanied soldiers E-5 and below are required to reside in the barracks and in-process with their unit and the barracks manager.

What is the JBLM Rental Partnership Program?

Military OneSource says the Housing Services Office program encourages landlords to give 5% or more off monthly rent and to waive security or rent deposits and credit-check fees. Call 253-967-3581 to ask about participating landlords.
