BAH in the National Capital Region: Pentagon, Fort Belvoir, Fort Myer (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at National Capital Region (also searched as Pentagon, Fort Belvoir, Fort Myer, Fort McNair). DTMO lists this housing area as WASHINGTON, DC METRO AREA (code DC053). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at National Capital Region

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near National Capital Region runs about $2,800–$3,800/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Arlington / Alexandria (22202)) run about $290–$600/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $3,090–$4,400/month.

Quick answers

Do the Pentagon, Fort Belvoir, Fort Myer and Fort McNair all get the same BAH?

Yes. ZIPs 22202 (Pentagon), 22060 (Fort Belvoir), 22211 (Fort Myer) and 20319 (Fort McNair) all map to the WASHINGTON, DC METRO AREA (DC053). Fort Meade and Fort Detrick are separate housing areas with different rates.

Does 2026 BAH at National Capital Region cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $2,800–$3,800 and utilities of about $290–$600 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($3,132) lands between −$1,268 and $42 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
