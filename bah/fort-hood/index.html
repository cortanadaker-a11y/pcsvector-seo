BAH at Fort Hood, TX (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Hood. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT HOOD, TX (code TX286). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Hood

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Hood runs about $1,250–$1,650/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Killeen (76541 / 76542)) run about $270–$510/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,520–$2,160/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Hood

Fort Hood’s housing office says most local housing is in Killeen, Harker Heights and Copperas Cove, 10 to 15 minutes from post, with Temple, Belton, Gatesville and Lampasas about 30 minutes away. Living on post is voluntary except for personnel in key and essential positions. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Hood

  1. In-process through the Housing Services Office (HSO) before you rent, lease or buy locally; this is mandatory. It is in the Copeland Soldier Service Center, Building 18010, B209, T.J. Mills Boulevard; phone 254-285-2313. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  2. Ask the HSO for its list of registered rental agencies, and for lease review or help with any discrimination complaint or landlord-tenant dispute. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  3. Budget for fees: local agencies and property managers require non-refundable administrative and rental/credit-history fees and a non-refundable pet deposit, and may require a security deposit equal to one month’s rent. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  4. Do not sign until the HSO has reviewed the property, and make sure the lease contains the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act clause, which lets you end the lease if orders take you away for more than 90 days. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  5. Complete the move-in inspection checklist within the allotted time and keep a signed copy to avoid penalty or repair charges when you leave. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  6. To live on post, apply within 30 days of arriving to receive an eligibility date; later applications use the application date. Listed wait times range from two weeks to 12 months depending on occupancy. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: privatized housing in 13 villages (single-family homes and townhomes, two to five bedrooms), operated by the installation’s housing partners; applications go through the partner websites or the HSO. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  • Off post: homes and apartments in Killeen, Harker Heights, Copperas Cove, Temple, Belton, Gatesville and Lampasas, all within a 30-minute drive of Fort Hood. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)

Quick answers

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Hood cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,250–$1,650 and utilities of about $270–$510 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,695) lands between −$465 and $175 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Hood in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Hood with dependents receives $1,695 a month and $1,530 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,340 with and $1,833 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT HOOD, TX (TX286). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.
