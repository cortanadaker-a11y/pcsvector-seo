BAH at Fort Benning (formerly Fort Moore), GA (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Benning (also searched as Fort Moore). DTMO lists this housing area as FORT BENNING, GA (code GA075). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Benning

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Benning runs about $1,300–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Columbus north (31909)) run about $260–$500/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,560–$2,250/month.

Quick answers

Is Fort Moore BAH different from Fort Benning BAH?

No. Fort Moore was the post’s name from 2023 to 2025. Same post, same housing area (GA075), same rates. Columbus, GA and Phenix City, AL (36867, 36869) are both in it.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Benning cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,300–$1,750 and utilities of about $260–$500 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,716) lands between −$534 and $156 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
