BAH at Fort Benning, GA (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Benning. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT BENNING, GA (code GA075). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Benning

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Benning runs about $1,300–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Columbus north (31909)) run about $260–$500/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,560–$2,250/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Benning

Fort Benning’s family housing is run by The Villages of Benning, and Military OneSource says it charges no credit check, background check, application fee or security deposit to active-duty military. Your place on the wait list is set by the day you cleared your last duty station, so start the application early. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Benning

  1. Sign in first. During duty hours permanent-party soldiers report to the Installation Replacement Operations Center, Ridgway Hall (Building 35), Room 239; after hours they report to post Staff Duty at McGinnis-Wickam Hall (Building 4). Military OneSource says not to report to your unit first unless you are a single soldier going to barracks. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning check-in procedures)
  2. Plan for a seven-day in-processing program touching medical, finance, transportation, housing and education. The first full day includes a four-hour briefing session that covers housing and finance, after which you get a schedule and a cohort leader. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning check-in procedures)
  3. Visit the Housing Services Office (HSO) in Ridgway Hall (Building 35), Room 385, phone 706-545-3921, for current off-post listings. Military OneSource lists hours of Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and Thursday 8 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)
  4. For on-post family housing, apply as soon as you can: your eligibility date is the date you signed out of your last PCS station, and it sets your place on the list but not how long you wait. Applicants who apply more than 30 days after arriving are listed with the date of their application instead. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)
  5. Bring complete documentation. Military OneSource says applicants with missing paperwork go on administrative hold and are not offered a home until everything is received, and that no availability date is guaranteed. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)
  6. Lodging while you wait: the on-post IHG hotel is Holiday Inn Express Abrams Hall (Building 167, 7350 Ingersoll Road). Military OneSource notes on-post lodging is often full, especially in summer, and Fort Benning Lodging helps with off-post rooms in Columbus. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning temporary housing)
  7. Single soldiers contact the Unaccompanied Housing Office (706-545-3921) or their prospective unit about barracks. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: family homes are managed by The Villages of Benning through six community management offices, with 24-hour emergency maintenance, mowing and curbside trash pickup. The Welcome Center and leasing office is at 601 Lumpkin Street, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., 706-685-3939. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)
  • Deposits and pets on post: no credit check, background check, application fees or security deposit for active-duty military, and no pet deposit for the first two approved pets. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)
  • School letters are issued at the housing appointment, but only if the wait for quarters is under 90 days. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)
  • Off post: the HSO is the contact for off-post tenant and landlord disputes and discrimination complaints, and it can point you to available temporary lodging while you wait for on-post housing. (Military OneSource, Fort Benning housing)

Quick answers

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Benning cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,300–$1,750 and utilities of about $260–$500 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,716) lands between −$534 and $156 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Benning in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Benning with dependents receives $1,716 a month and $1,482 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,058 with and $1,878 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT BENNING, GA (GA075). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Does The Villages of Benning charge a deposit?

Military OneSource’s Fort Benning housing page says the Villages offer no credit check, background check, application fee or security deposit for active-duty military, and no pet deposit for the first two approved pets. Confirm current terms with the leasing office before you apply.

Does my place on the Fort Benning housing wait list start when I arrive?

No. Military OneSource says your eligibility date is the date you signed out of your last PCS station, but applicants who apply more than 30 days after arriving are listed with their application date, so apply early.
