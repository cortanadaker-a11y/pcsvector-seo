BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Carson

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Typical off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Carson runs about $1,750–$2,400/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Fountain / Widefield (80817)) run about $255–$525/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $2,005–$2,925/month.

Quick answers

Why does DTMO call it Colorado Springs?

DTMO sets BAH by Military Housing Area, and Fort Carson sits in the COLORADO SPRINGS, CO area (CO046). Peterson SFB, Schriever SFB and the Air Force Academy share the same area and the same rates.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Carson cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With typical 3-bedroom rent of $1,750–$2,400 and utilities of about $255–$525 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,358) lands between −$567 and $353 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
