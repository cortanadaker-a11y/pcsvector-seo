Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Polk

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Typical off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Polk runs about $1,050–$1,500/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Leesville (71446)) run about $265–$515/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,315–$2,015/month.

Quick answers

Is Fort Johnson BAH different from Fort Polk BAH?

No. Fort Johnson was the post’s name from 2023 to 2025. Same post, same housing area (LA115), same rates. Leesville (71446) and DeRidder (70634) are both in it.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Polk cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With typical 3-bedroom rent of $1,050–$1,500 and utilities of about $265–$515 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,218) lands between −$797 and −$97 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
