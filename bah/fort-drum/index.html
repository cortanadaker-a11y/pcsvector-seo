BAH at Fort Drum, NY (2026)

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Drum

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Drum runs about $1,300–$1,700/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Evans Mills / Le Ray (13637)) run about $330–$690/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,630–$2,390/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Drum

At Fort Drum, one fact changes the BAH math: Military OneSource says the rental rate for on-post family housing covers utilities, weekly trash and recycling, and 24-hour emergency maintenance. Off post you pay utilities yourself, which in the North Country means heat. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Drum

  1. Visit the Housing Services Office / Army Community Housing, Clark Hall, 10720 Mount Belvedere Boulevard, 2nd floor, room A2-54. Phone 315-772-6668; listed hours Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  2. The HSO keeps a list of rentals near Fort Drum, updated weekly, and helps with discrimination and landlord-tenant disputes. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  3. Expect most off-post homes to be unfurnished with a 12-month lease, plus a security deposit, first month’s rent and a pet deposit; furnished apartments are hard to find. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  4. Insist on a military clause that lets you end the lease early under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act if you deploy or get PCS orders, and have the HSO or the Legal Assistance Office review the lease. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  5. If you want on-post family housing, start the advance application early with Fort Drum Mountain Community Homes (315-955-6644). (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  6. Need a four- or five-bedroom home? Military OneSource advises allowing more time and widening the search to single-family rentals, buying, or places farther from post. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Fort Drum Mountain Community Homes maintains 3,793 homes, two to five bedrooms, with garages, private outdoor space and washer and dryer hookups. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  • Single members: those at E-5 and below who are not entitled to with-dependents BAH must live in the barracks; The Timbers has 192 one- and two-bedroom apartments for single and unaccompanied members at E-6 and above. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  • Off post: apartments, duplexes, single homes and mobile homes; Fort Drum itself has no mobile home park. (Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)

Quick answers

Why do Fort Drum utilities matter so much?

Winter heat. In the North Country, propane, oil or gas heat can push a 3-bedroom’s monthly utilities past $350 in the coldest months. Ask a landlord for last winter’s bills before you sign.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Drum cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,300–$1,700 and utilities of about $330–$690 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,665) lands between −$725 and $35 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Drum in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Drum with dependents receives $1,665 a month and $1,422 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,469 with and $1,902 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT DRUM/WATERTOWN, NY (NY225). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.
