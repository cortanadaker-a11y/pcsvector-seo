BAH at Fort Huachuca, AZ (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Huachuca. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT HUACHUCA, AZ (code AZ014). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Huachuca

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Huachuca runs about $1,250–$1,750/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Sierra Vista (85635)) run about $260–$505/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,510–$2,255/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Huachuca

Fort Huachuca’s family housing is privatized under the Army’s Residential Communities Initiative and managed by Mountain Vista Communities. Military OneSource says the housing and management offices share one building, and that you must sign in for duty and hold PCS orders before you can apply for an on-post home. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Huachuca

  1. Enter through the Van Deman Gate Welcome Center (open Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5:15 p.m.) or the Buffalo Soldier Gate, which is open 24 hours. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca check-in procedures)
  2. In-processing starts at your unit or activity, which books you into the Personnel Processing Center briefing on Tuesdays 7:30 a.m. to noon (908 Butler Road, Building 22420). Newcomers Orientation is Wednesdays 8 a.m. to noon at Murr Community Center (Building 51301, Cushing Street). (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca check-in procedures)
  3. Report to the Housing Services Office (HSO) as part of in-processing if you have family members. Military OneSource says it is required, and that you need your sign-in for duty and PCS orders before applying for on-post housing. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)
  4. The HSO and Mountain Vista Communities offices are both in Building 52065 on Smith Street, Monday to Friday 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., phone 520-515-9000. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)
  5. Reserve lodging early: IHG Army Hotels’ Candlewood Suites takes reservations up to a year ahead (520-226-3800, 24 hours), pet rooms are limited and in high demand, and PCS and TDY travelers pay a portion of the Cochise County per diem, no more than the full amount. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca temporary housing)
  6. Do not wait on a Statement of Non-Availability: Military OneSource says none is needed or possible, because the lodging is privately owned. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca temporary housing)
  7. If you have pets, plan for the Pet Addendum that Mountain Vista requires, and check the restricted-breed list before you commit. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)

Housing options at a glance

  • On-post family housing is one- and two-story single-family homes and duplexes, and some homes date from 1884 in the old cavalry fort, now a state and national historic landmark. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)
  • The Army’s partner plans 1,064 privatized family units in a 50-year development, with new construction and renovation of existing homes. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)
  • Homes are open to members of all service branches assigned to Fort Huachuca; waiting time varies by rank and bedroom requirement. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)
  • Pets: two per home; dogs of listed aggressive breeds (Pit Bull types, Rottweilers, Doberman Pinschers, Chows and wolf hybrids) are not permitted, and certified assistance animals are not pets under the policy. (Military OneSource, Fort Huachuca housing)

Quick answers

Is Fort Huachuca BAH the same in Sierra Vista?

Yes. Sierra Vista (85635) is in the FORT HUACHUCA, AZ housing area (AZ014). BAH follows your duty-station ZIP anyway, not the town you rent in.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Huachuca cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,250–$1,750 and utilities of about $260–$505 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,719) lands between −$536 and $209 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Huachuca in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Huachuca with dependents receives $1,719 a month and $1,347 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,232 with and $2,019 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT HUACHUCA, AZ (AZ014). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

Do I need a non-availability statement for Fort Huachuca lodging?

Military OneSource’s Fort Huachuca temporary housing page says that if lodging is unavailable a Statement of Non-Availability is not needed or possible, because the hotel is privately owned. Ask the lodging office and your finance office how your lodging reimbursement works.

When can I apply for on-post housing at Fort Huachuca?

Military OneSource says military personnel must sign in for duty and have their PCS orders before they can apply for on-post housing, and that all members with families must report to the Housing Services Office as part of in-processing.
