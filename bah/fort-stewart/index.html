BAH at Fort Stewart, GA (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Stewart. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT STEWART, GA (code GA080). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Stewart

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Stewart runs about $1,200–$1,550/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Hinesville (31313)) run about $270–$515/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,470–$2,065/month.

PCS guide: moving to Fort Stewart

Fort Stewart’s on-post rent is tied directly to your BAH: Military OneSource says the rent allotment for Fort Stewart Family Homes equals the soldier’s BAH entitlement for their rank. It also says every soldier reporting to the installation must process through the Housing Service Office before renting or buying off post. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)

PCS checklist for Fort Stewart

  1. Sign in at the installation’s reception center on Fort Stewart, even if you are assigned to Hunter Army Airfield, unless you have specific directions otherwise. Expect a few days of in-processing, and submit the proper leave request before taking permissive leave for house hunting. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart check-in procedures)
  2. Hand-carry list: PCS orders with amendments, DA Form 31, military ID cards, marriage certificate, divorce decree, birth certificates, a household goods inventory and any other military records. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart check-in procedures)
  3. Process through the Housing Service Office (HSO) before renting or buying off post. Military OneSource says it provides rental lists, lease reviews, inspections for suitability, mediation of landlord disputes and information on the deposit waiver program. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  4. Apply for family housing online (or fax a printed application). When you arrive, bring your military ID, Leave and Earnings Statement, copy of orders and a DEERS form or birth certificates for every child in the home. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  5. Apply within 30 days of leaving your last station to keep your eligibility date: Military OneSource says it is the day you left, otherwise the date of your application. From an unaccompanied tour such as Korea, you get credit from the date you departed for that tour, up to 14 months, with orders as proof. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  6. If you are married and this is your first duty station, bring your Basic Training and AIT orders to confirm your eligibility date. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  7. Concurrent travel with family must be authorized on your PCS orders. If you marry before you PCS or report, Military OneSource says follow the procedures exactly or the military will not pay for your spouse’s travel and housing. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart check-in procedures)
  8. Book lodging early: Army Lodging (Building 4951, across from Club Stewart) takes PCS reservations up to a year ahead, with a 30-day first stay and extensions on request. It has 70 double rooms and 99 single rooms. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart temporary housing; Military OneSource, Fort Stewart check-in procedures)

Housing options at a glance

  • On post: Fort Stewart Family Homes, managed by Balfour Beatty Communities, has 3,268 air-conditioned homes in 10 neighborhoods, from two to five bedrooms (912-408-2466). (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  • Most homes are for active-duty Army families assigned to Fort Stewart; the page says the highest proportion is for E-1 to E-6 and the lowest for officers, and rental homes are also open to retirees, DoW civilians and the public. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  • Pets: two are allowed in family housing at no extra charge; restricted breeds and exotic pets such as snakes, rodents and reptiles are not allowed. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  • Exceptional family members: adaptable and wheelchair-accessible homes are available, but the page says generators are not provided for medical equipment. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)
  • There are no mobile home parks on post and no government-leased housing in the community. (Military OneSource, Fort Stewart housing)

Quick answers

Does Hunter Army Airfield get Fort Stewart BAH?

No. Hunter Army Airfield (ZIP 31409) is in Savannah, which DTMO puts in a separate housing area, SAVANNAH, GA (GA077), with its own rates. Fort Stewart and Hinesville (31313) are in FORT STEWART, GA (GA080).

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Stewart cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,200–$1,550 and utilities of about $270–$515 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($2,310) lands between $245 and $840 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.

What is BAH at Fort Stewart in 2026?

In 2026 an E-5 (Sergeant) at Fort Stewart with dependents receives $2,310 a month and $1,947 without dependents; an O-3 receives $2,598 with and $2,385 without. DTMO lists the housing area as FORT STEWART, GA (GA080). Every pay grade is in the table on this page.

How much rent will I pay in Fort Stewart Family Homes?

Military OneSource says the rent allotment equals the soldier’s Basic Allowance for Housing entitlement for their rank, so check the Fort Stewart BAH table on this page for your grade and dependent status. Ask the leasing office what utilities and fees are included.

When does my Fort Stewart housing wait-list date start?

Military OneSource says that if you apply within 30 days of leaving your previous duty station, your eligibility date is the day you left; otherwise it is your application date. A completed application is required to join the wait list.
