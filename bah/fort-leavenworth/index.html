BAH at Fort Leavenworth, KS (2026)

Monthly Basic Allowance for Housing for Soldiers stationed at Fort Leavenworth. DTMO lists this housing area as FORT LEAVENWORTH, KS (code KS102). Your rate depends on three things only: pay grade, with or without dependents, and your duty-station ZIP. Where you choose to live does not change it.

BAH vs rent and utilities at Fort Leavenworth

BAH is a flat monthly amount; rent and utilities are not. Avg off-post 3-bedroom rent near Fort Leavenworth runs about $1,350–$1,850/month, and utilities for that home (electric, heat, water/trash, internet; Leavenworth (66048)) run about $260–$515/month. That puts all-in housing at roughly $1,610–$2,365/month.

Quick answers

Do Leavenworth and Platte City get the same BAH?

Yes. The city of Leavenworth (66048) and Platte City, MO (64079) are both in the FORT LEAVENWORTH, KS housing area (KS102). BAH follows your duty-station ZIP anyway, not the town you rent in.

Does 2026 BAH at Fort Leavenworth cover rent and utilities?

It depends on rank. With avg 3-bedroom rent of $1,350–$1,850 and utilities of about $260–$515 a month, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents ($1,815) lands between −$550 and $205 a month after housing. These are planning estimates, not official figures.

Do I get a different rate if I live in a nearby town?

No. BAH follows your permanent duty-station ZIP, not your home address.

When do these rates apply?

These are the calendar-year 2026 rates. Rate protection keeps your rate from dropping while you stay at the same post with the same status; a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status resets it. DTMO usually publishes the next year’s rates in mid-December.
