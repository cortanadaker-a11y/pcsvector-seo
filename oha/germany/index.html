“With dependents” is yes or no for the rent ceiling. O-1E to O-3E are officers with more than four years of prior enlisted or warrant service. OHA pays your actual rent up to the ceiling, never more.

Moving to Germany: do not sign anything before the housing brief

Every garrison in Germany says the same thing in different words: attend the housing brief and in-process first, and have the housing office review the lease before you sign it. German rental law also makes verbal agreements binding.

Who can live off post

  • USAG Bavaria states the Army in Europe assignment policy: service members on PCS orders are mandatorily assigned to government housing to maintain optimum occupancy. (USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • USAG Rheinland-Pfalz (Kaiserslautern, Baumholder) says Soldiers of all ranks are required to live on post until a 95% occupancy rate is reached, that private rental needs a statement of non-availability or an exception to policy, and that the wait for government housing is one to eight weeks depending on bedrooms. In the Kaiserslautern Military Community the Air Force Housing Office manages accompanied housing. (USAG Rheinland-Pfalz Housing Services Office)
  • Unaccompanied E-1 to E-6 at Kaiserslautern live in barracks; E-7 and above are entitled to off-post housing and report to the Air Force Housing Office (Vogelweh Housing, Building 1001). (USAG Rheinland-Pfalz Housing Services Office)

Wiesbaden checklist

  • Report to the Housing Office within 48 hours of arrival, attend the in-person Housing Brief (Wednesdays at 09:00, Building 1023W, Room 210 per the garrison page; schedule subject to change), complete in-processing, then start house-hunting. (USAG Wiesbaden off-post housing)
  • Before you arrive, browse HOMES.mil to compare home sizes and prices and decide how much furniture to ship or store. (USAG Wiesbaden off-post housing)
  • Negotiate and sign the lease at the Housing Services Office with the landlord and a counselor; bilingual agreements are preferred. The April 2022 briefing warns that verbal agreements in Germany are binding and that termination requires written notice with a 3-month period. (USAG Wiesbaden off-post housing briefing (Apr 2022))
  • Ask the counselor whether your landlord is on the Non-Referral or Restricted Sanction list; the briefing says signing with a landlord on the Restricted Sanctions List (entries are for validated discrimination) is not recommended. (USAG Wiesbaden off-post housing briefing (Apr 2022))
  • Complete a move-in inspection. A garrison article advises recording any damage on the inspection form with photos and giving the landlord a copy, so move-out goes smoothly. (USAG Wiesbaden off-post housing; Army.mil, Avoid problems with your landlord (Wiesbaden))

Bavaria (Grafenwoehr, Vilseck, Hohenfels) checklist

  • Do not enter a rental contract, written or verbal, before the housing brief and in-processing. Use HOMES.mil, searching “USAG Bavaria”. (USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • Contract review: you and the landlord sign the contract cover sheet, then you schedule a review with the housing office within two business days of the landlord signing; the appointment takes about 60 minutes on average. (USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • How OHA is paid: the garrison says you receive the lesser of your rent or the rank-based rent cap, plus a monthly utility allowance. MIHA includes a one-time miscellaneous payment for items such as transformers and curtains; realtor fees may be authorized for special housing needs (for example five or more bedrooms or Exceptional Family Member needs). (USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • Army members can request an advance housing allowance for the first month’s rent and security deposit, which the page says typically equals two months’ rent; take your signed contract to your unit S1. (USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • Know the house rules: landlords may choose their tenants (including meeting a dog), and quiet hours run 10 p.m. to 6 a.m., 1 to 3 p.m., and all day Sundays and German federal holidays. (USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • Square-meter prices are high and some units cost more than the OHA rent cap; apartments, townhouses and duplexes are generally less expensive and easier to find than large single-family homes. (USAG Bavaria private rental)

Not covered here

  • Stuttgart and Ansbach have the same OHA tables above, but this page has no verified housing-office rules for them; ask the USAG Stuttgart or USAG Ansbach housing office before signing.

How OHA works

OHA has three parts. Rent: you are reimbursed your actual monthly rent, up to a rent ceiling set by DTMO for your grade, dependency status and locality. Utilities: a flat monthly utility/recurring maintenance allowance for the locality, paid on top of rent. Move-in: a one-time Move-In Housing Allowance (MIHA) toward the costs of setting up a private rental.

Ceilings are set in the local currency. Your rent is in local currency, and finance converts it to dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, so the dollar amount moves with the exchange rate even when your rent does not. DTMO can update exchange rates and OHA rates every pay period, on the 1st and 16th of each month. Members without dependents get 90% of the with-dependents rent ceiling and 75% of the utility allowance.

Quick answers

How much OHA does an E-5 get at Wiesbaden?

As of 1 Nov 2026 DTMO lists a rent ceiling of $2,802.78 (€2,460) for a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents and $2,522.50 without, plus a utility allowance of $1,138.20 with dependents and $853.65 without. With dependents that is up to $3,940.98 a month.

Do Grafenwoehr and Vilseck get the same OHA?

Yes. DTMO uses one locality, VILSECK/GRAFENWOEHR (DE231), for both. Other Bavarian areas such as Ansbach (DE228) have their own ceilings.

Why does my OHA change in dollars when my rent stays the same?

Ceilings and your lease are in local currency. Finance pays you in dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, which DTMO can update every pay period (the 1st and 16th), so the dollar amount rises or falls with the exchange rate.

Do Soldiers without dependents get OHA?

Yes, if they are authorized to live off post. The without-dependents rent ceiling is 90% of the with-dependents ceiling and the utility allowance is 75%. Soldiers in government quarters or the barracks do not get OHA.
