Moving to Korea: housing-office rules before you sign

OHA only pays if you are authorized to live off post, and in Korea that is decided by the garrison housing office, not by you or the landlord. These are the rules the housing offices themselves publish.

Is off-post housing open to you?

  • Camp Humphreys: the Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024) says off-post housing is authorized only if the occupancy rate for government housing is above 95%, and that personnel on unaccompanied tours receiving the dependent-location BAH rate must live in available unaccompanied housing. The garrison’s housing page says about 40 percent of command-sponsored military families at Humphreys live on post. (USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024); USAG Humphreys DPW Housing Division)
  • Camp Casey and Camp Hovey: the USAG Yongsan-Casey page on Military OneSource states, “No Military Personnel will be authorized to live off post at CP Casey/Hovey, as UPH Quarters are available.” The Camp Casey OHA table on this page is shown for reference; confirm with the Casey housing office before you plan around it. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing)

Order of operations at Camp Humphreys

  • Do not make lease arrangements without prior approval from the Housing Office. Attend the mandatory off-post housing briefing (Tuesday and Thursday at 1400 per the Oct 2024 sheet) before you deal with realtors, and bring your PCS orders and amendments. (USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024))
  • Use the USAG Humphreys Registered Realtor List. Every off-post home must pass a safety inspection by the Housing Office, which you can schedule through the realtor. (USAG Humphreys Off-Post In-processing Packet, Army (PDF dated 20 Feb 2024); USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024))
  • Select housing within 15 days of arrival: the sheet says TLA can be terminated for failure to do so without a valid reason. (USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024))
  • Lease processing is by appointment only; the housing office warns that TLA or TQSA stops on the effective lease start date, and that after signing, changes or addenda need Housing Office approval. (USAG Humphreys Off-Post In-processing Packet, Army (PDF dated 20 Feb 2024))
  • Arrange government loaner furniture before household goods delivery; the sheet says to schedule the furniture pickup before arranging HHG delivery and that each member is entitled to one pickup during the tour. (USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024); USAG Humphreys Off-Post In-processing Packet, Army (PDF dated 20 Feb 2024))

Lease terms to check anywhere in Korea

  • Bring the realtor or landlord to the housing office before signing so the unit’s fair market value is checked; the safety inspection must be done before the lease is signed. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing)
  • Rent is paid in won while OHA is paid in dollars, and a move-in housing allowance (MIHA) is paid on signing the lease; you may also be eligible for dislocation allowance. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing; DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • Give the landlord written notice at least 30 days before you terminate, and make sure all bills are paid before termination. A military clause protects you under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (30 days’ written notice after receiving orders, per the Humphreys HSO booklet). (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing; Humphreys Housing Services Office booklet)
  • Electricity is billed in tiers, so usage drives cost: Military OneSource says $1,000 a month for electricity alone is “not unreasonable” in summer if energy is not conserved, and members with negotiated utility caps should monitor usage. Compare that with the utility allowance in the table before you accept a lease without caps. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing)
  • Apartment management fees (security, cleaning, elevators) are typically billed with electricity, water and oil; water off post is not potable for cooking or drinking. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing)

Facts above are quoted or paraphrased from the official pages below, read on 28 Oct 2026. Garrison pages change; confirm with the housing office before you sign. For how the allowance itself is calculated, see How OHA works for an overseas PCS.

How OHA works

OHA has three parts. Rent: you are reimbursed your actual monthly rent, up to a rent ceiling set by DTMO for your grade, dependency status and locality. Utilities: a flat monthly utility/recurring maintenance allowance for the locality, paid on top of rent. Move-in: a one-time Move-In Housing Allowance (MIHA) toward the costs of setting up a private rental.

Ceilings are set in the local currency. Your rent is in local currency, and finance converts it to dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, so the dollar amount moves with the exchange rate even when your rent does not. DTMO can update exchange rates and OHA rates every pay period, on the 1st and 16th of each month. Members without dependents get 90% of the with-dependents rent ceiling and 75% of the utility allowance.

Quick answers

How much OHA does an E-5 get at Camp Humphreys?

As of 16 Oct 2026 DTMO lists a rent ceiling of $1,458.51 (₩1,888,889) for a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents and $1,312.66 without, plus a utility allowance of $824.84 with dependents and $618.63 without. With dependents that is up to $2,283.35 a month.

Do Camp Humphreys and Osan AB get the same OHA?

Yes. DTMO puts Camp Humphreys in the OSAN AB (INCL CAMP HUMPHREYS) locality (KR025), so the same rent ceilings and utility allowance apply. Camp Casey is a different locality, DONGDUCHEON (INCL CAMP CASEY) (KR177), with lower ceilings.

Why does my OHA change in dollars when my rent stays the same?

Ceilings and your lease are in local currency. Finance pays you in dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, which DTMO can update every pay period (the 1st and 16th), so the dollar amount rises or falls with the exchange rate.

Do Soldiers without dependents get OHA?

Yes, if they are authorized to live off post. The without-dependents rent ceiling is 90% of the with-dependents ceiling and the utility allowance is 75%. Soldiers in government quarters or the barracks do not get OHA.
