The MIHA figure is what DTMO’s calculator shows for a member with dependents; DTMO did not return a MIHA figure for the without-dependents query, so check with your housing office. Dollar figures are DTMO’s conversion at the rate shown; local-currency amounts are rounded to whole units.

“With dependents” is yes or no for the rent ceiling. O-1E to O-3E are officers with more than four years of prior enlisted or warrant service. OHA pays your actual rent up to the ceiling, never more.

How OHA works

OHA has three parts. Rent: you are reimbursed your actual monthly rent, up to a rent ceiling set by DTMO for your grade, dependency status and locality. Utilities: a flat monthly utility/recurring maintenance allowance for the locality, paid on top of rent. Move-in: a one-time Move-In Housing Allowance (MIHA) toward the costs of setting up a private rental.

Ceilings are set in the local currency. Your rent is in local currency, and finance converts it to dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, so the dollar amount moves with the exchange rate even when your rent does not. DTMO can update exchange rates and OHA rates every pay period, on the 1st and 16th of each month. Members without dependents get 90% of the with-dependents rent ceiling and 75% of the utility allowance.

Quick answers

How much OHA does an E-5 get at Okinawa?

As of 16 Oct 2026 DTMO lists a rent ceiling of $1,979.95 (¥300,000) for a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents and $1,781.96 without, plus a utility allowance of $739.26 with dependents and $554.44 without. With dependents that is up to $2,719.21 a month.

Is Okinawa OHA higher than mainland Japan?

At this pay period the Okinawa rent ceilings are higher than Camp Zama, Yokota and Yokosuka for the same grade; the utility allowance is the same. See the Japan page for the mainland localities.

Why does my OHA change in dollars when my rent stays the same?

Ceilings and your lease are in local currency. Finance pays you in dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, which DTMO can update every pay period (the 1st and 16th), so the dollar amount rises or falls with the exchange rate.

Do Soldiers without dependents get OHA?

Yes, if they are authorized to live off post. The without-dependents rent ceiling is 90% of the with-dependents ceiling and the utility allowance is 75%. Soldiers in government quarters or the barracks do not get OHA.
