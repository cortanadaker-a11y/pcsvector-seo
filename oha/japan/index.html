Moving to Camp Zama: why OHA may not apply to you

The OHA table below is DTMO’s rate for the locality. Whether you receive it at Camp Zama depends on a housing assignment policy that is stricter than most U.S. posts.

Camp Zama housing policy

  • USAG Japan states that family and unaccompanied housing in Japan is built by the Government of Japan under the SOFA and managed by the garrison, so military personnel are mandatorily assigned to family housing or unaccompanied housing. (USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing)
  • The Camp Zama housing FAQ says you can live off post only with an approved Exception to Policy (ETP), and that housing under the Mandatory On-Post Housing Assignment Policy is assigned to incoming members when available. (Camp Zama housing FAQ)
  • Military OneSource describes a different group: accompanied service members at E-7 and above assigned by orders to Sagami General Depot, Yokohama North Dock, NAF Atsugi and Akasaka Press Center (installations other than Camp Zama proper) may reside off post without an ETP, and unaccompanied officers and E-7 to E-9 reside off base unless in key and essential positions. The two pages are not worded the same way, so ask the Housing Office which applies to your orders. (Military OneSource, Camp Zama government housing)

Order of operations

  • Report to the Housing Office, Building 671, within 2 working days of arriving in Japan. Family housing applications use DD Form 1746 plus your orders and DA 31 or DA 137-2; no advance applications are accepted. (USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing)
  • Do not secure off-base housing before you arrive: the garrison says all off-post housing must be approved by the Housing Office to meet DoD suitability standards. (USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing; Military OneSource, Camp Zama government housing)
  • The FAQ lists a typical wait for family housing of 30 to 60 days (subject to change) and says lodging is available meanwhile, but the garrison says an extension of a hotel stay because of a delayed housing assignment will not be authorized. (Camp Zama housing FAQ; USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing)
  • If you are approved to live off post you receive OHA instead of BAH, and the dollar amount varies with the yen rate. (Camp Zama housing FAQ)

What changes in the move

  • Voltage in Japan is 100V at 50 Hz, so check appliances before shipping. (USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing)
  • Camp Zama is a weight-restricted area: the garrison says to ask your Transportation Office for your authorized allowance, and that furnishings that do not fit are stored at your expense. The Housing Office provides limited full-tour furniture, and Army Community Service has a lending closet for kitchen basics. (USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing)
  • This page also lists Yokota and Yokosuka rates from DTMO, but those installations’ off-post rules are not covered here; use their own housing offices.

How OHA works

OHA has three parts. Rent: you are reimbursed your actual monthly rent, up to a rent ceiling set by DTMO for your grade, dependency status and locality. Utilities: a flat monthly utility/recurring maintenance allowance for the locality, paid on top of rent. Move-in: a one-time Move-In Housing Allowance (MIHA) toward the costs of setting up a private rental.

Ceilings are set in the local currency. Your rent is in local currency, and finance converts it to dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, so the dollar amount moves with the exchange rate even when your rent does not. DTMO can update exchange rates and OHA rates every pay period, on the 1st and 16th of each month. Members without dependents get 90% of the with-dependents rent ceiling and 75% of the utility allowance.

Quick answers

How much OHA does an E-5 get at Camp Zama?

As of 16 Oct 2026 DTMO lists a rent ceiling of $1,759.96 (¥266,667) for a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents and $1,583.96 without, plus a utility allowance of $739.26 with dependents and $554.44 without. With dependents that is up to $2,499.22 a month.

Is the Japan utility allowance the same at every base?

For these three localities, yes as of this pay period: DTMO lists the same utility/recurring maintenance allowance and the same move-in housing allowance for Camp Zama, Yokota and Yokosuka. Rent ceilings differ by locality and grade.

Why does my OHA change in dollars when my rent stays the same?

Ceilings and your lease are in local currency. Finance pays you in dollars at the DTMO exchange rate, which DTMO can update every pay period (the 1st and 16th), so the dollar amount rises or falls with the exchange rate.

Do Soldiers without dependents get OHA?

Yes, if they are authorized to live off post. The without-dependents rent ceiling is 90% of the with-dependents ceiling and the utility allowance is 75%. Soldiers in government quarters or the barracks do not get OHA.
