How OHA works for an overseas PCS

The Overseas Housing Allowance (OHA) replaces BAH when you are authorized to rent off post overseas. The formula is different from BAH, and so are the traps: the amount moves with exchange rates, and the rules about who may rent off post depend on the garrison. This guide explains how DTMO builds OHA, and ties it to the 2026 tables on our Korea, Japan, Okinawa and Germany pages. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)

The three parts of OHA

  • OHA is a cost-reimbursement allowance for members assigned to permanent duty overseas who lease privately owned housing. It has three components: rent, utilities/recurring maintenance, and a move-in housing allowance (MIHA). (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)

Rent: a ceiling, not a flat payment

  • Rental allowances are computed from actual rent payments reported through local finance systems. The maximum is set so that 80 percent of members with dependents have their rent fully reimbursed, so some members pay part of the rent themselves. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • All members are reimbursed for rent up to the amount of the lease or the allowance, whichever is less. Members without dependents are entitled to 90 percent of the with-dependents rate. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • Bavaria’s garrison puts it the same way for its members: you receive OHA based on your rent or the rank-based rent cap, whichever is less. (USAG Bavaria private rental)

Utilities and recurring maintenance

  • The monthly utility/recurring maintenance allowance covers utilities paid directly to utility companies plus recurring maintenance and minor repairs. It is set to cover the 80th percentile of costs reported by members who pay their own utility bills. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • If your landlord pays the utilities you do not receive this allowance; it is added to your rent allowance instead. Members without dependents who pay their own utilities get 75 percent of the with-dependents rate. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)

Move-in housing allowance (MIHA)

  • MIHA/Miscellaneous is an up-front lump sum for items that make a dwelling habitable (DTMO’s examples: transformers, supplemental heating equipment, wardrobes, electricity connection), paid with your first OHA rent payment to members with and without dependents. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • MIHA/Rent is a dollar-for-dollar reimbursement of reasonable fixed, one-time, nonrefundable charges the landlord or a foreign government imposes before or when you occupy the home. Refundable deposits, advance rent and recurring costs are not included, and homeowners are ineligible. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • Other components: MIHA/Security, MIHA/Infectious Disease and MIHA/Safety apply only in locations DTMO designates for them. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)

Applying for OHA

  • You apply by completing DD Form 2367 (Individual OHA Report) and returning it with a copy of your lease to the appropriate official; MIHA claims use DD Form 2556. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)
  • Authorization to live off post comes first and is decided locally. Korea, Japan and Germany garrisons each publish their own rules; see the housing-office sections on the Korea, Japan and Germany OHA pages. (USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024); USAG Japan, moving in: on-post / off-post housing; USAG Bavaria private rental)

OHA unique expenses

  • OHA Unique Expenses reimburse excessive housing costs that a CONUS-based member does not normally have, with receipts, in listed locations. DTMO’s table lists France (mandatory habitation tax) and Denmark (refurbishing quarters on departure). (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance)

Quick answers

Does OHA cover all of my rent overseas?

Not always. DTMO sets the maximum rent allowance so that 80 percent of members with dependents have rent fully reimbursed, and you are reimbursed up to the lease amount or the allowance, whichever is less. Some members pay part of the rent.

Why did my OHA change when my rent did not?

OHA is paid in U.S. dollars but rent is typically paid in foreign currency, so the dollar amount changes with the exchange rate even if the local-currency rent is the same. DTMO updates rates on a pay-period schedule.

What is MIHA?

The Move-In Housing Allowance partially covers move-in costs for a privately leased home. The miscellaneous part is a lump sum paid with the first rent payment, and the rent part reimburses reasonable one-time nonrefundable charges.

Do I get OHA if I live in government housing overseas?

OHA applies to members authorized to lease privately owned housing. Garrisons such as Camp Zama assign military personnel to government housing and allow off-post living only with an exception to policy, so check with the housing office before you plan around the table.
