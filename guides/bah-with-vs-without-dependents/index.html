BAH with vs without dependents (2026)

The difference between BAH with and without dependents is not a fixed amount. It depends on the post and the pay grade, and at some posts it is several hundred dollars a month. This guide uses the 2026 DTMO rates already on our BAH pages to show the gap at each post, sorted by the E-5 gap.

See your own with/without numbers in the calculator →

The rules that set the gap

  • BAH distinguishes between with-dependents and without-dependents, not the number of dependents. One child and four children get the same with-dependents rate. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • Rate protection ends if your dependency status changes, the same as for a PCS or a reduction in grade, so a marriage, divorce or first child resets which rate you are protected at. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • DTMO says out-of-pocket housing costs can be higher or lower than the rate depending on the home you choose; BAH is a planning number, not a promise that rent is covered. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)

Quick answers

Does BAH go up with each child?

No. DTMO pays one with-dependents rate whether you have one dependent or several; the only split is with versus without dependents.

How big is the with-dependents gap for an E-5?

Across the 27 BAH posts on this site, a Sergeant (E-5) with dependents gets between $90 and $807 a month more than without, using DTMO's 2026 rates.

Does getting married change my BAH at the same post?

Yes. A change in dependency status moves you to the with-dependents rate for that post and ends the rate protection tied to your old status. Report the change to finance with the required documents.
