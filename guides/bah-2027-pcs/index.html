BAH 2027 and your PCS: what we know

If you are planning a PCS this winter, you will probably see headlines about 2027 BAH before you see the numbers. As of 28 Oct 2026 DTMO has not published 2027 rates, and PCS Vector shows no 2027 amounts. This guide covers what is known, what the rules say about a PCS, and how to use the 2026 pages as a baseline. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)

What we know about the next BAH release

  • BAH rates are set per calendar year. For 2026, coverage of the Defense Department’s 11 Dec 2025 release says new rates averaged a 4.2 percent increase (versus 5.4 percent in 2025) and took effect 1 Jan 2026, with increases varying by location. (Air & Space Forces Magazine, 2026 BAH and COLA release coverage (30 Dec 2025))
  • The same coverage says members who kept BAH eligibility in places where rates fell were not cut, because rate protection exists so members with long-term commitments are not penalized. (Air & Space Forces Magazine, 2026 BAH and COLA release coverage (30 Dec 2025); DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • DTMO says rental prices generally change 2% to 5% from year to year and housing allowances typically change accordingly. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • DTMO’s BAH page does not give a 2027 publication date. Based on last year’s release (mid-December), plan for new rates around then, and treat any 2027 figure you see before that as unofficial. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing; Air & Space Forces Magazine, 2026 BAH and COLA release coverage (30 Dec 2025))

What a PCS does to your rate

  • Rate protection keeps you from receiving less than the previous year’s rate while your BAH status stays the same. It ends if your status changes through a PCS, a reduction in grade or a change in dependency status. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • After you arrive at the new duty station, rate protection applies again: you get any published increase but no decrease while your status stays the same. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • BAH distinguishes between with-dependents and without-dependents, not the number of dependents. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)

Using the 2026 pages as a baseline

Look up your new post, note the E-5, E-6 or O-3 figure for your grade, then compare it with the planning rent range on that page. When DTMO publishes, the new numbers replace these, but the gap between BAH and local rent changes by far less than the rate on its own does, because rents move as well. Quick answers

When are 2027 BAH rates published?

DTMO has not published 2027 rates as of 28 Oct 2026. The 2026 rates were announced in a Defense Department release dated 11 Dec 2025 and took effect 1 Jan 2026, so plan for new rates in mid-December.

Will my BAH drop if rates fall at my post?

Not if your status stays the same. DTMO’s individual rate protection means you receive the rate published on 1 January or what you received on 31 December, whichever is larger, until a PCS, reduction in grade or change in dependency status.

Which BAH do I use if I PCS right after 1 January?

Use your new duty station’s published rate for the year it applies to, and check it against DTMO’s BAH calculator for your ZIP code. This site shows the 2026 rates until DTMO publishes 2027.
