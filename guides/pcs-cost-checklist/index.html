PCS cost checklist: what you pay up front and what comes back

A PCS has two cash-flow problems: money that comes back later, and money you hand over first. This checklist separates them, with a cited source page (DTMO, Military OneSource or the installation) behind every line. Where a number depends on your rank, check the BAH page for your new post.

Reimbursements and allowances to claim

  • ☐ Dislocation allowance (DLA) partially reimburses household-moving expenses for a PCS. You typically qualify if you move your household on PCS orders and are not assigned to single-type government housing such as barracks; most members receive only one DLA per fiscal year. (Military OneSource, Dislocation Allowance FAQs)
  • ☐ DLA amount depends on pay grade and dependency status; DTMO publishes the rates. File at your installation finance office. (Military OneSource, Dislocation Allowance FAQs)
  • ☐ You can request a DLA advance at your former installation’s finance office 10 to 15 days before the move. If approved you get 80% of the estimated payment in advance and the rest after you file at the new installation; approval is not guaranteed. (Military OneSource, Dislocation Allowance FAQs)
  • ☐ Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) partially reimburses lodging and meals in temporary lodging inside CONUS during a PCS. You cannot receive both TLE and PCS per diem on the same day. Ask your local pay office. (Military OneSource, Temporary Lodging Expense)

Costs you front before the first BAH check

  • ☐ Security deposit. Fort Sill’s page says deposits vary and often equal one month’s rent; Fort Campbell’s says usually one month’s rent or less, or none, and that it is not an advance on your last month’s rent. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing; Military OneSource, Fort Campbell housing)
  • ☐ Application and credit-check fees. Fort Campbell’s housing office warns these add up if you are denied; Fort Hood’s says local agencies require non-refundable administrative and rental/credit-history fees. (Military OneSource, Fort Campbell housing; Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing)
  • ☐ Pet deposit. Fort Hood: non-refundable. Fort Drum: expect security deposit, first month’s rent and a pet deposit with a typical 12-month lease. (Military OneSource, Fort Hood housing; Military OneSource, Fort Drum housing)
  • ☐ First month’s rent. In Korea, the Humphreys booklet says in most cases the landlord requires at least the first month’s rent along with a security deposit unless negotiated; get a receipt. (Humphreys Housing Services Office booklet)
  • ☐ Renters insurance. Fort Sill, JBSA and the Korea housing offices recommend it, because the owner’s policy does not cover your belongings. (Military OneSource, Fort Sill housing; Military OneSource, JBSA housing; Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing)
  • ☐ Utility deposits and connection: Military OneSource’s installation pages point to InMyArea.com to compare providers by ZIP code. (Military OneSource, Fort Campbell housing; Military OneSource, JBSA housing)

Ways to cut the up-front bill

  • ☐ Fort Campbell’s Rental Partnership Program aims to reduce the initial cost of renting through reduced or waived deposits, discounted rent and lower application fees; it requires a military allotment. (Military OneSource, Fort Campbell housing)
  • ☐ Fort Riley’s Army Housing Office may be able to help get a deposit waived, depending on the property owner. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)

How the housing allowance changes with the PCS

  • ☐ BAH is not meant to cover every housing cost; DTMO says out-of-pocket costs can be higher or lower than the rate depending on the home you choose, and that rental prices generally change 2% to 5% a year. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)
  • ☐ Rate protection: a PCS ends your old rate protection. At the new station you receive any published increase but no decrease while your status stays the same. (DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing)

If the move is overseas

  • ☐ Overseas you also have MIHA (a lump sum with the first OHA payment plus reimbursement of reasonable one-time landlord charges). Bavaria’s garrison says army members may request an advance housing allowance for the first month’s rent and security deposit, which it says typically equals two months’ rent. (DTMO, Overseas Housing Allowance; USAG Bavaria private rental)
  • ☐ At Camp Humphreys an advance of rent or security deposit uses DA Form 4187 signed by the company commander, hand-carried to finance with a copy of the DD 2367. (Humphreys Housing Services Office booklet)
  • ☐ Temporary lodging allowance (TLA) is approved by the housing office and processed by finance; Humphreys says it stops on the effective lease start date. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing; USAG Humphreys Off-Post In-processing Packet, Army (PDF dated 20 Feb 2024))

Quick answers

Do I get a security deposit back after a PCS?

It depends on the lease. Fort Campbell’s housing office says a deposit may cover damage above normal wear and tear or unpaid rent, and that if the rental is in good shape and no rent is due you should ask for it back; it is not an advance on your last month’s rent.

Can I get my DLA before I move?

You can request an advance 10 to 15 days before the move at your installation finance office. If approved you receive 80% of your estimated payment up front and the rest after you file at the new installation, but an advance is not guaranteed.

Does BAH cover everything about renting near a new post?

No. DTMO says BAH is not intended to cover all of a member’s housing costs, so budget deposits, fees and utilities separately. The BAH pages on this site compare each post’s rate to planning estimates for rent and utilities.
