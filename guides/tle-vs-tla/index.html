TLE vs TLA on a military PCS

Calculate your BAH →

TLE vs TLA: TLE (Temporary Lodging Expense) partially reimburses temporary lodging and meals inside the continental United States during a PCS, while TLA (Temporary Lodging Allowance) is the OCONUS lodging allowance for duty outside the 48 contiguous states, including Alaska and Hawaii, and the day limits published by housing offices are not the same at every post.

TLE: temporary lodging inside the United States

  • Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) partially reimburses lodging and meals in temporary lodging inside CONUS during a PCS. You cannot receive both TLE and PCS per diem on the same day. Ask your local pay office. (Military OneSource, Temporary Lodging Expense)
  • Fort Riley’s housing page tells arrivals to secure temporary lodging before they arrive and to be ready to pay for lodging beyond what the Temporary Lodging Expense covers until adequate housing is found. (Military OneSource, Fort Riley housing)

TLA: lodging outside the 48 states (OCONUS)

TLA is the allowance for OCONUS duty, outside the 48 contiguous states, including Alaska and Hawaii. It is not one number. These are the limits already published on our installation pages, each tied to the housing-office source.

  • Temporary lodging allowance (TLA) is approved by the housing office and processed by finance. At Camp Humphreys it stops on the effective lease start date. (Military OneSource, USAG Yongsan-Casey housing; USAG Humphreys Off-Post In-processing Packet, Army (PDF dated 20 Feb 2024))
  • The Humphreys housing sheet says to select housing within 15 days of arrival, and that TLA can be terminated for failure to do so without a valid reason. (USAG Humphreys Housing Information Sheet (Oct 2024))
  • Fort Wainwright: request TLA within the first five days. Only the Housing Services Office can authorize it. It comes in 10-day increments with proof of an active housing search, for up to 20 days, and the home must be ready within 60 days of sign-in. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright temporary housing; Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright check-in procedures)
  • At Fort Wainwright, TLA is not extended because of pet-housing difficulties. The Housing Services Office can help find pet-friendly places. (Military OneSource, Fort Wainwright housing)
  • Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson: Military OneSource says the maximum is 45 days, an extension is not automatic, and claims are submitted every 15 days with proof of at least one home viewing a day. TLA is not authorized for single, unaccompanied service members. (Military OneSource, Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson temporary housing)
  • Schofield Barracks: the Housing Service Office (808-787-7700) gives a list of TLA-approved facilities. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter temporary housing)
  • Schofield Barracks: Military OneSource describes officer-housing waits up to the 60-day TLA limit. (Military OneSource, Schofield Barracks and Fort Shafter housing)

Quick answers

Is TLE the same thing as TLA?

No. TLE is the Temporary Lodging Expense for temporary lodging inside the continental United States during a PCS. TLA is the Temporary Lodging Allowance for OCONUS duty, outside the 48 contiguous states, including Alaska and Hawaii, and the day limits published by housing offices are not the same at every post.

Can I receive TLE and PCS per diem on the same day?

No. Military OneSource says you cannot receive both TLE and PCS per diem on the same day. Ask your local pay office how your orders are paid.

How many days of TLA do I get?

There is no single number. Fort Wainwright caps TLA at 20 days in 10-day increments. Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson caps it at 45 days. Schofield Barracks describes a 60-day TLA limit for officer-housing waits. Camp Humphreys says TLA can stop if housing is not selected within 15 days, and it stops on the lease start date.

Does this site publish one TLE day limit?

No. Military OneSource describes TLE as partial reimbursement for CONUS temporary lodging and meals, and says you cannot take it on the same day as PCS per diem. Fort Riley tells arrivals to budget for lodging beyond what TLE covers. Confirm the day count with your pay office.
